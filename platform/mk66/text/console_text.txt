# OS-FLASHDIET P5/P8: the console's long texts, one per line: <key><TAB><text as a C string literal body>. tools/text_pack.py packs them into one deflate blob (text.h `text(txt::<key>)`).
# The text is exactly what the C++ source had as a literal: the served bytes are the old ones (tests/mk66_text_test.cpp). The `help_<command>` keys are the one-line helps of command_table.h.
