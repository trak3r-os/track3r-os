# OS-MANUAL2 C4: the "Try it" of docs/manual/pattern_tools.md. Each `snap walk_<n>` is the picture of step n, `pattern_tools_walk_<n>.png` in docs/manual/img/ (manifest docs/manual/images.txt).
# The pad and F-key presses are the chapter's own.

# Setup, not a step: the empty project (File > NEW, ALL), so the walk-through starts on one empty pattern, as a new project does.
document new
wait 20
tap file
wait 20
tap f3
wait 20
tap f3
wait 700
assert state screen=PATTERN
wait 3000

# Step 1. Press `Pattern`, then `Rec`. The chip reads STEP. No picture: the Pattern chapter shows it.
wait 700
tap rec
wait 20
assert state screen=PATTERN

# Step 2. Hold `Shift` and press `Up` on step 1: the whole track is selected. Press `F4` (FILL). The Fill dialog opens on WHAT NOTE / TYPE EVERY N, and its footer says where the fill goes: TRACK 1  ROWS 1-64.
press shift
tap up
release shift
wait 20
tap f4
wait 20
snap walk_2

# Step 3. `Down` once and `Right` twice: TYPE reads EUCLID. `Down` and `Right` once more: EVENTS 5 (from 4). The line shows the five hits.
tap down
tap right 2
tap down
tap right
wait 20
snap walk_3

# Step 4. Press `F1` (FILL). The notes land on the five hits, the dialog closes, the strip says FILL: EUCLID 5 EVENTS.
tap f1
wait 20
snap walk_4

# Step 5. Press `F7` (the verb menu). The sixteen verbs are on the pads, DUPLICATE, EXPAND and SHRINK among them. No picture: step 6 shows the result.
wait 3000
tap f7
wait 20

# Step 6. Press pad 11 (EXPAND x2). Each note moves to every second step and the strip names the new length.
pad 10
wait 20
snap walk_6

# Step 7. Press `F8` (UNDO). The length comes back. No picture.
tap f8
wait 20
