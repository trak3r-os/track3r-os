# OS-MANUAL2 C3: the walk-through of the Song chapter's "Try it" (docs/manual/song.md): copy a slot, share a pattern, make it your own, look at the timeline, undo.
# Each `snap walk_<n>` is the picture of step n, `song_walk_<n>.png` in docs/manual/img/ (manifest: docs/manual/images.txt). A changed step is a changed picture: edit both together.
# `document` opens the demo project (one slot, pattern 1).

# Step 1. Press `Song`. The LIST page lists the slots; the cursor is on the first.
document
wait 30
tap song
wait 20
assert state screen=SONG
snap walk_1

# Step 2. Press `Shift` + `Insert` (DUP PAT): a new slot after the cursor, holding a copy of the pattern under a new number. The info line says SHIFT+INS DUP.
press shift
tap insert
release shift
wait 20
snap walk_2

# Step 3. Press `F3` (ADD SLOT): one more slot that holds the same pattern number as the cursor's. Two slots share it: the * mark and "pattern nn in 2 slots".
tap f3
wait 20
snap walk_3

# Step 4. Press `F5` (UNIQUE): the cursor's slot gets a copy of its own. The strip says SLOT nn: PATTERN nn (A COPY OF nn).
tap f5
wait 20
snap walk_4

# Step 5. Press `Song` again: the TIMELINE page draws the same slots as blocks as wide as they have bars. Press `Song` once more to come back to the list.
tap song
wait 20
snap walk_5
tap song
wait 20

# Step 6. Press `F8` (UNDO): the slot points at the shared pattern again.
tap f8
wait 20
snap walk_6
