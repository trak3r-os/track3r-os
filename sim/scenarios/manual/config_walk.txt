# OS-MANUAL2 C5: the walk-through of the Config chapter's "Try it" (docs/manual/config.md). Each `snap walk_<n>` is the picture of step n, `config_walk_<n>.png` in docs/manual/img/
# (manifest: docs/manual/images.txt; tools/manual_images.py renders it, ctest o3sim_manual_images keeps the committed pictures in step). A new step or a changed key means a changed picture: edit the
# chapter's step and this line together. Nothing here is written to the card: the USB value is only pending. No engine is needed.

# Step 1. Press `Config`. The Config screen opens.
tap config
wait 20
assert state screen=CONFIG

# Step 2. Press `F6` (PATTERN). The ten rows of the PATTERN page show, the cursor on the first.
tap f6
wait 20
snap walk_2

# Step 3. Press `Down` five times (the cursor is on METRO) and turn the encoder one click right: the row reads Main.
tap down 5
encoder 1
wait 20

# Step 4. Turn the encoder two clicks left: the row reads Off.
encoder -2
wait 20
snap walk_4

# Step 5. Press `F5` (USB) and turn the encoder one click left: USB AUDIO reads Off with a star, and the info line says it is not applied yet.
tap f5
encoder -1
wait 20
snap walk_5

# Step 6. Press `F1` (MIDI), then `F5` again: the pending Off is gone, USB AUDIO reads On with no star.
tap f1
tap f5
wait 20
snap walk_6
