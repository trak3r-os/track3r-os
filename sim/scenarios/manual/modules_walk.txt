# OS-MANUAL2 C2: the walk-through of the Modules chapter's "Try it" (docs/manual/modules.md). Each `snap walk_<n>` is the picture of step n, `modules_walk_<n>.png` in docs/manual/img/.
# Edit the chapter's step and this line together.
#
# The Navigator route the chapter writes (press `Song` and `Pattern` together, encoder to MODULES, `F8` GO) ends in the action the scenario runs (`nav.screen 12`, as qa_modules does).
# `card e2e_card` and `apps on` give the simulated card the shipped apps (sim/include/o3os/sim/sim_apps.h). Nothing is played.
action nav.screen 12
document
wait 20
card e2e_card
apps on

# Step 1. Open the Modules screen and press `F2` (RESCAN): the apps on the card are listed, each with its state.
tap f2
wait 50
assert state screen=MODULES
snap walk_1
wait 2500

# Step 2. Put the cursor on MIDI Monitor (the fourth row) and press `F1` (LAUNCH). It only listens, so it starts at once; its key row names its keys, and `F8` reads BACK.
tap down 3
tap f1
wait 100
assert state screen=APP
snap walk_2

# Step 3. Press `F8` (BACK). You are on the list again.
tap f8
wait 50
assert state screen=MODULES

# Step 4. Put the cursor on Rhythm Gen (the seventh row) and press `F1`. It edits the project, so it asks first: F1 reads LAUNCH? and the strip says what it will do.
tap down 3
tap f1
wait 50
assert state screen=MODULES
snap walk_4

# Step 5. Press `F1` again. Rhythm Gen starts.
tap f1
wait 100
assert state screen=APP
snap walk_5

# Step 6. Press `Song`. The app ends and the Song screen opens; the app never sees the key.
tap song
wait 50
assert state screen=SONG
