# OS-SONG3 S3 and S5 (docs/specs/OS-SONG3.md; new Community OS behaviour): the rest of S3 on the LIST (the dimmed muted map cells, the playing row's progress line) and S5's ADD EMPTY. QA-SNG-209, QA-SNG-210, QA-SNG-211, docs/qa/catalogue/song.md. Goldens are keyed qa_song_s3/<snap>.
# Two tracks muted on the Mixer (F7 on the first two strips), back on Song's LIST with the map, the song playing, and ADD EMPTY on Copy held + F3.
document
audio on
wait 20
tap master
tap f1
tap f7
tap right
tap f7
tap song
assert state screen=SONG page=0

# QA-SNG-209 (OS-SONG3 S3): a muted track's map cell on the LIST is dim (TextDim), the unmuted tracks' cells keep their ink; the rule reads the track's own mute flag only, never a solo.
snap qa_sng_209

# QA-SNG-210 (OS-SONG3 S3, spec section 3.4 item 7): while the song plays, a thin Play line under the PAT tile of the playing row runs with the playhead through the slot's pattern; stopped, there is none.
tap play
wait 700
assert state playing=1
snap qa_sng_210
tap play
wait 100
assert state playing=0

# QA-SNG-211 (OS-SONG3 S5, Q6 d): Copy held + F3 (ADD EMPTY) adds a slot after the cursor's on the first free pattern number (the pattern does not exist: the slot is empty and its PAT tile is dim), the cursor on it, one undo unit; F8 takes it back.
press copy
tap f3
release copy
wait 20
assert model order.len=2  # the demo song has one slot; ADD EMPTY made the second
snap qa_sng_211
tap f8
assert model order.len=1  # F8 took the empty slot back
