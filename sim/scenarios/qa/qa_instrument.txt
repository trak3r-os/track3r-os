# OS-FINALQA (docs/specs/OS-FINALQA.md): the Instrument screen's QA rows, docs/qa/catalogue/instrument.md. One `snap qa_ins_NNN` per row with a visible result; the goldens are keyed qa_instrument/qa_ins_NNN.
# OS-SHIFTROW: with Shift held the key row shows the Shift layer: a label only on a key that does something, "-" elsewhere; Shift + F never falls through to the plain key.
