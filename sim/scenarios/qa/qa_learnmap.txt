# OS-LEARNMAP (docs/specs/OS-LEARNMAP.md): the LEARN MAP overlay, docs/qa/catalogue/global.md (QA-GLB-234..240). One `snap qa_lmap_NNN` per row with a visible result;
# the goldens are keyed qa_learnmap/qa_lmap_NNN. The sim has no knob, so a CC is the `midicc` line; the activity dot lasts 250 ms, so a snap right after a `midicc` shows it.
