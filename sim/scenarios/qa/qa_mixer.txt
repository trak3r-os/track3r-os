# OS-FINALQA (docs/specs/OS-FINALQA.md): the Mixer screen's QA rows, docs/qa/catalogue/mixer.md. One `snap qa_mix_NNN` per row with a visible result; the goldens are keyed qa_mixer/qa_mix_NNN.
# OS-SHIFTROW: with Shift held the key row shows the Shift layer: a label only on a key that does something, "-" elsewhere; Shift + F never falls through to the plain key.

# QA-MIX-001: the Mixer with Shift held: - - - - - - UNMUTE UNSOLO.
tap master
press shift
snap qa_mix_001
release shift

# QA-MIX-002 (OS-MIXHIDE): the MASTER FX page (F3) shows only the limiter: the card at the right reads MASTER LIMITER / ALWAYS ON, with none of the reserved stages (headroom, saturation, EQ, bass, space, bit depth), and no LIMITER on/off row (OS-SMALL3: the limiter is always on).
tap master
tap f3
snap qa_mix_002

# QA-MIX-030 (OS-MASTERVOL): Master held + the encoder is the master volume, from any screen. On Pattern the strip says MASTER VOLUME 79% (the demo document's 86, seven detents down); the screen does not change and the release is silent.
# The next plain tap of Master (it acts at the release) is the Mixer, whose MST column shows 79.
wait 3000
document
tap pattern
press master
wait 5
encoder -7
snap qa_mix_030
assert state screen=PATTERN
release master
wait 5
assert state screen=PATTERN
tap master
assert state screen=MIXER
snap qa_mix_030_mixer

# OS-MIDICCMAP S2 (docs/specs/OS-MIDICCMAP.md, section 4 S2): the fixed CC map, gated by the CC MAP device setting (Config > MIDI, after IN CHANNEL; Off by default).
# QA-MIX-078: CC MAP On, then `midicc 1 71 64` on the Pattern screen sets track 1's level to 50 (64 * 100 / 127, written at once); the Mixer's T1 column shows 50.
document
tap config
tap f1
tap up 16
tap down 10
encoder 1
tap pattern
midicc 1 71 64
wait 3000
tap master
tap f1
