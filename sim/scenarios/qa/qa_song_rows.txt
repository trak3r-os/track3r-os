# A row claims only what an assert here checks; the rest stays under "open" in the catalogue cell.

# QA-SNG-012 (OS-DELREPEAT): a held Delete removes one slot. A song of four slots, the cursor on the last
# one; Delete is held for 1.5 s of simulated time (long enough for key repeats) and the list is three slots,
# not fewer. The same for F4 held.
document
tap song
tap f3
tap f3
tap f3
assert model order.len=4
press delete
wait 1500
release delete
wait 20
assert model order.len=3
press f4
wait 1500
release f4
wait 20
assert model order.len=2
