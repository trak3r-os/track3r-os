# OS-CEWTENV W7a / W7b (QA-INS-294..303): the MOD page's WT ENV and WT LFO sources, slot 3 as Gran Length on a granular instrument, and the contour drawing. A file of its own: the contours are made into the card's bank (a run has one card, and qa_instrument.txt opens another later). The goldens are qa_instrument_wt/qa_ins_NNN.

document
wait 20
card e2e_card
action instrument.select 1
tap params
wait 8000
# F4 opens the MOD page (DEST 1/6), F4 again is the next destination: DEST 2/6, the pan.
tap f4 2
# QA-INS-294: SOURCE turned three clicks up from Off reads WT ENV (Off, ENV, LFO, WT ENV); the page has eight rows, SOURCE at the top.
encoder +3
assert model inst.1.mod1.source=3
snap qa_ins_294
# QA-INS-295: the WT ENV rows: WAVE (the contour, F or U and its number), DURATION (10 ms a click), WARP X, WARP Y, SUSTAIN (OFF at 0, then 1..99 %).
tap down
encoder +10
tap down
encoder +50
tap down
encoder +3
tap down
encoder -2
tap down
encoder +50
assert model inst.1.wt1.wave=12
assert model inst.1.wt1.duration=502
assert model inst.1.wt1.warp_x=3
assert model inst.1.wt1.warp_y=-2
assert model inst.1.wt1.sustain=50
snap qa_ins_295
# QA-INS-296: one F8 takes back the last change only (SUSTAIN back to OFF); the warps and the duration stay.
tap f8
assert model inst.1.wt1.sustain=0
assert model inst.1.wt1.warp_y=-2
snap qa_ins_296
# QA-INS-297: SOURCE one more click up is WT LFO: six rows (SOURCE, WAVE, SPEED, WARP X, WARP Y, AMOUNT); the contour and the warps are the ones just set.
tap up 5
encoder +1
assert model inst.1.mod1.source=4
assert model inst.1.wt1.wave=12
snap qa_ins_297
# QA-INS-298: SPEED is the LFO's own row (a click moves it); the contour is shared with WT ENV (one byte per destination).
tap down 1
tap down 1
encoder +4
snap qa_ins_298
# QA-INS-299: back to LFO the SHAPE row reads a Triangle (the stored contour byte stays in the file and plays as a Triangle), and a WT slot's rows are gone.
tap up 2
encoder -2
assert model inst.1.mod1.source=2
tap down
snap qa_ins_299

# OS-CEWTENV W7b (QA-INS-300..303): slot 3 on a granular instrument and the WT contour drawing, on the fourth destination (DEST 4/6), two F4 presses on from the pan destination.
tap f4 2
tap up 3
action instrument.play_mode 7
# QA-INS-300: on a GRANULAR instrument the destination is Gran Length (the header and the rows are lit, no WAVETABLE OR GRANULAR line); SOURCE one click up is Envelope.
encoder +1
assert model inst.1.mod3.source=1
snap qa_ins_300
# QA-INS-301: SOURCE two clicks on is WT Env: the plot is the contour (F001, a saw rise, as the default WAVE is 0) over its duration, with the length in ms above the right edge.
encoder +2
assert model inst.1.mod3.source=3
snap qa_ins_301
# QA-INS-302: the warps bend the picture: WARP X +5 and WARP Y -4 (the rows are SOURCE, WAVE, DURATION, WARP X, WARP Y, ...).
tap down 3
encoder +5
tap down
encoder -4
assert model inst.1.wt3.warp_x=5
assert model inst.1.wt3.warp_y=-4
snap qa_ins_302
# QA-INS-303: WT LFO draws the same contour over one cycle (the cue reads ONE CYCLE).
tap up 4
encoder +1
assert model inst.1.mod3.source=4
snap qa_ins_303
