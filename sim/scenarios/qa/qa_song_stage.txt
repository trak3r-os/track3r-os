# OS-SONG-STAGE (docs/specs/OS-SONG-VIEW.md): the Song screen's STAGE page, QA-SNG-067..072, docs/qa/catalogue/song.md. Goldens are keyed qa_song_stage/<snap>.
# OS-SONG-STAGE (docs/specs/OS-SONG-VIEW.md; new Community OS behaviour): the Song button's fourth page, STAGE. QA-SNG-067: stopped, the cursor's slot is CURSOR and the next slot NEXT, the countdown line says STOPPED.
document
audio on
wait 20
tap song
tap f3
tap f3
tap up 2
tap song
tap song
tap song
assert state screen=SONG page=3
snap qa_sng_067

# QA-SNG-068: playing, the slot that plays is NOW (the green block), NEXT is the slot after it, the countdown says the bars left and the progress bar and the BAR n OF m line follow the playhead.
tap play
wait 700
assert state playing=1
snap qa_sng_068

# QA-SNG-069: a live jump (Rec with the cursor on another slot while the song plays) replaces NEXT: captioned JUMP, an orange frame, the countdown line JUMP IN.
tap down 2
tap rec
wait 20
snap qa_sng_069

# QA-SNG-070: at the song's last slot NEXT is slot 0, captioned LOOP, the countdown line LOOP IN (stopped: the cursor on the last slot).
tap play
wait 100
press shift
tap f5
release shift
snap qa_sng_070

# QA-SNG-072: the page key turns the pages in order: ORDER, ARRANGEMENT, TIMELINE, STAGE, back to ORDER (the header says n/4); a peek of Song from another screen shows the page it was left on.
tap song
assert state screen=SONG page=0
snap qa_sng_072
tap song
tap song
tap song
tap master
press song
wait 500
assert state screen=SONG page=3
snap qa_sng_072_peek
release song
wait 5
assert state screen=MIXER
