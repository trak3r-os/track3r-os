# OS-UXF row 2, the Focus Loop (docs/specs/OS-UXF.md): QA-PAT-540 to 543 on the real engine (`audio on`: the sequencer behind the app, so the order of the start and the loop on the command ring is the one
# the unit runs). The sound document is an empty 16-step pattern at 120 BPM (125 ms a step, a 2 s pass). A block of rows 5 to 12 (NUMBERING counts from 1; model rows 4..11), Shift + Play from stop:
# the info line says LOOP 05-12 and goes on saying it after more than one pass of the pattern, because the line wraps at the block's end; Shift + Play again stops and the line is bare.
document sound
audio on
wait 30
tap rec
assert state mode=0 playing=0
action cursor.track 0
action cursor.jump_fraction 0
action cursor.home
tap down
tap down
tap down
tap down
action select.extend 1
action select.extend 1
action select.extend 1
action select.extend 1
action select.extend 1
action select.extend 1
action select.extend 1
wait 10
assert state screen=PATTERN playing=0

# QA-PAT-540: Shift + Play with the block selected starts the pattern at the block's first row and loops its last; the strip says the rows. Two and a half passes of the 16-step pattern later it still loops.
press shift
tap play
release shift
wait 100
assert state playing=1
snap qa_pat_540_start
wait 4900
assert state playing=1
snap qa_pat_540_later

# QA-PAT-541: Shift + Play again stops (no jump, no new loop); the transport is stopped and the loop line is gone.
press shift
tap play
release shift
wait 100
assert state playing=0
snap qa_pat_541_stopped
