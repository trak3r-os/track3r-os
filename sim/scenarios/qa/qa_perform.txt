# OS-FINALQA (docs/specs/OS-FINALQA.md): the Perform screen's QA rows, docs/qa/catalogue/perform.md. One `snap qa_prf_NNN` per row with a visible result; the goldens are keyed qa_perform/qa_prf_NNN.
# OS-SHIFTROW: with Shift held the key row shows the Shift layer: a label only on a key that does something, "-" elsewhere; Shift + F never falls through to the plain key.

# QA-PRF-001: the Perform screen with Shift held: STORE 1 STORE 2 STORE 3 - - - - - (the marks under the keys stay).
tap performance
press shift
snap qa_prf_001
release shift

document sound
