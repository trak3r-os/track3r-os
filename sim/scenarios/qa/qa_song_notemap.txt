# OS-SONG-VIEW2 V4 (docs/specs/OS-SONG-VIEW2.md; new Community OS behaviour): the note map of the Song screen's TIMELINE page, QA-SNG-088 and 090, docs/qa/catalogue/song.md. Goldens are keyed qa_song_notemap/<snap>.
# QA-SNG-088: the note map. Under each block one lane a track (the demo's twelve), a tick where a step holds a note, sixteen steps to a bar: the demo's four-bar pattern and its
# three copies (Shift + Insert) show the same ticks; the last two lanes (the external line tracks) hold volume and FX only and have none.
document
tap song
press shift
tap insert
tap insert
tap insert
release shift
tap up 3
tap song
tap song
assert state screen=SONG page=2
snap qa_sng_088
