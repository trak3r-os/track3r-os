# OS-SONG-TIMELINE (docs/specs/OS-SONG-VIEW.md): the Song screen's TIMELINE page, QA-SNG-073..082, docs/qa/catalogue/song.md. Goldens are keyed qa_song_timeline/<snap>.
# OS-SONG-TIMELINE (new Community OS behaviour): the Song button's third page. QA-SNG-073: four slots (patterns 1 to 4) as blocks as wide as they have bars, a colour and the pattern's number on each, the thumbnails of the
# tracks' note density under them, the cursor's block in a white frame; the header says TIMELINE 3/4 and the info line the cursor's slot, its start bar, pattern and length.
document
audio on
wait 20
tap song
press shift
tap insert
tap insert
tap insert
release shift
tap up 3
tap song
tap song
assert state screen=SONG
snap qa_sng_073

# QA-SNG-076: Left and Right (like Up and Down) walk the cursor slot along the page; the frame, the info line and the window follow.
tap right 2
tap down
snap qa_sng_076

# QA-SNG-077: playing, the slot that plays has a green bar under its block and a white playhead line crosses its block and the lanes at the playing step.
tap up
tap play
wait 700
assert state playing=1
snap qa_sng_077
