# OS-SONG-TIMELINE (docs/specs/OS-SONG-VIEW.md): the Song screen's TIMELINE page, QA-SNG-073..082 and 083..087 (OS-SONG-VIEW2), docs/qa/catalogue/song.md. Goldens are keyed qa_song_timeline/<snap>.
# OS-SONG-TIMELINE (new Community OS behaviour): the Song button's third page. QA-SNG-073: four slots (patterns 1 to 4) as blocks as wide as they have bars, a colour and the pattern's number on each, the thumbnails of the
# tracks' note density under them, the cursor's block in a white frame; the header says TIMELINE 3/3 and the info line the cursor's slot, its start bar, pattern and length.
document
audio on
wait 20
tap song
press shift
tap insert
tap insert
tap insert
release shift
tap up 3
tap song
tap song
assert state screen=SONG
snap qa_sng_073

# QA-SNG-076: Left and Right (like Up and Down) walk the cursor slot along the page; the frame, the info line and the window follow.
tap right 2
tap down
snap qa_sng_076

# QA-SNG-077: playing, the slot that plays has a green bar under its block and a white playhead line crosses its block and the lanes at the playing step.
tap up
tap play
wait 700
assert state playing=1
snap qa_sng_077

# QA-SNG-083 (OS-SONG-VIEW2 V2; the countdown that was STAGE's): while it plays the info line starts with the bars left in the playing pattern, NEXT IN n BARS, and counts down; the cursor's slot follows.
wait 2800
assert state playing=1
snap qa_sng_083

# QA-SNG-084: a live jump (Rec with the cursor on another slot while the song plays) replaces NEXT with JUMP: the info line says JUMP IN n BARS (the JUMP QUEUED message covers it for its first two seconds).
tap down 2
tap rec
wait 2500
snap qa_sng_084

# QA-SNG-085: playing the song's last slot the info line says LOOP IN n BARS (the song loops to slot 0).
tap play
wait 100
press shift
tap f5
release shift
tap play
wait 300
assert state playing=1
snap qa_sng_085

# QA-SNG-086: stopped, the info line is the cursor's slot, its start bar, pattern and length (no countdown).
tap play
wait 100
assert state playing=0
snap qa_sng_086

# QA-SNG-087: the page key turns ORDER, ARRANGEMENT, TIMELINE (the header says n/3), back to ORDER; a peek of Song from another screen shows the page it was left on.
tap song
assert state screen=SONG page=0
snap qa_sng_087
tap song
tap song
assert state screen=SONG page=2
tap master
press song
wait 500
assert state screen=SONG page=2
snap qa_sng_087_peek
release song
wait 5
assert state screen=MIXER
