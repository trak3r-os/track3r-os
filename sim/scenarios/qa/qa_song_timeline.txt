# OS-SONG-TIMELINE (docs/specs/OS-SONG-VIEW.md): the Song screen's TIMELINE page, QA-SNG-073..082 and 083..087 (OS-SONG-VIEW2), docs/qa/catalogue/song.md. Goldens are keyed qa_song_timeline/<snap>.
# OS-SONG-TIMELINE (new Community OS behaviour): the Song button's second page. QA-SNG-073: four slots (patterns 1 to 4) as blocks as wide as they have bars, a colour and the pattern's number on each, the thumbnails of the
# tracks' note density under them, the cursor's block in a white frame; the header says TIMELINE 2/2 (OS-SONG3 S1) and the info line the cursor's slot, its start bar, pattern and length.
document
audio on
wait 20
tap song
press shift
tap insert
tap insert
tap insert
release shift
tap up 3
tap song
assert state screen=SONG page=1
snap qa_sng_073

# QA-SNG-208 (OS-SONG3 S3): the shared mark on the blocks. F3 adds a slot after the cursor's with its pattern, so the two slots that hold pattern 1 both wear the `*` at the right of the block's number (the cursor's pattern's slots in the Fx ink, the other shared ones TextDim); F4 takes the slot back.
tap f3
assert model order.len=5
assert model order.0=1
assert model order.1=1  # the two slots that hold pattern 1: both wear the `*` on their blocks
snap qa_sng_208
tap f4
assert model order.len=4

# QA-SNG-076: Left and Right walk the cursor slot along the page; the frame, the info line and the window follow. (OS-SONG3 S2: Up and Down are the tracks now, QA-SNG-116.)
tap right 3
snap qa_sng_076

# QA-SNG-077: playing, the slot that plays has a green bar under its block and a white playhead line crosses its block and the lanes at the playing step.
tap left
tap play
wait 700
assert state playing=1
snap qa_sng_077

# QA-SNG-083 (OS-SONG-VIEW2 V2; the countdown that was STAGE's): while it plays the info line starts with the bars left in the playing pattern, NEXT IN n BARS, and counts down; the cursor's slot follows.
wait 2800
assert state playing=1
snap qa_sng_083

# QA-SNG-084: a live jump (Rec with the cursor on another slot while the song plays) replaces NEXT with JUMP: the info line says JUMP IN n BARS (the JUMP QUEUED message covers it for its first two seconds).
tap right 2
tap rec
wait 2500
snap qa_sng_084

# QA-SNG-085: playing the song's last slot the info line says LOOP IN n BARS (the song loops to slot 0).
tap play
wait 100
press shift
tap f5
release shift
tap play
wait 300
assert state playing=1
snap qa_sng_085

# QA-SNG-086: stopped, the info line is the cursor's slot, its start bar, pattern and length (no countdown).
tap play
wait 100
assert state playing=0
snap qa_sng_086

# QA-SNG-087: the page key turns LIST and TIMELINE (the header says n/2), back to LIST; a peek of Song from another screen shows the page it was left on.
tap song
assert state screen=SONG page=0
snap qa_sng_087
tap song
assert state screen=SONG page=1
tap master
press song
wait 500
assert state screen=SONG page=1
snap qa_sng_087_peek
release song
wait 5
assert state screen=MIXER
