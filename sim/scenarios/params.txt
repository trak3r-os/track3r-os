# OS-002d: the generated parameter page (Instrument PARAMS) and MIDI Learn. The page is made from the descriptor tables: the encoder edits the
# row under the cursor, Shift + encoder is the coarse step, Delete the default, F2..F5 the pages (F1 is ENGINE, the picker of the SD modules), F8 undo. A MIDI control change is `midicc <channel>
# <cc> <value>`: with the Learn overlay up it binds the cc to the row under the cursor, otherwise it moves what is bound (pickup: no jumps).

# No project yet: the defaults, and the page says so; an edit says why it did nothing.
tap params
snap no_project
tap down
encoder 3
snap no_project_edit
wait 2500

# The demo document (ten instruments, two bindings: CC 74 on instrument 1's cutoff, CC 71 on its resonance) and the VOICE page.
document bindings
snap voice
assert model inst.1.name=KICK
assert model inst.1.panning=26
encoder -12
snap voice_volume
assert model inst.1.panning=14
tap down 2
encoder 5
snap voice_tune
assert model inst.1.fine_tune=-15
press shift
encoder 1
release shift
snap voice_coarse
assert model inst.1.fine_tune=-5
tap delete
snap voice_reset
assert model inst.1.fine_tune=0
wait 2500

# The FILTER page: the response curve follows the values. The cutoff is bound to CC 74 in pickup mode, at 30 %: the knob has to reach it.
tap f3
tap down 2
snap filter
midicc 1 74 100
midicc 1 74 60
snap pickup_waiting
midicc 1 74 38
midicc 1 74 90
snap pickup_caught
tap f8
tap f8
snap pickup_undone
assert model inst.1.cutoff=0.3
wait 2500

# OS-LEARNFOLLOW S2: with the overlay up on a bound instrument parameter, the key row names F5 FOLLOW; F5 makes the binding follow the selected instrument (the
# target line reads INS * and the binding text ends FOLLOW, the strip says FOLLOWS), F5 again pins it back to the target's instrument (INS 03, PINNED).
press vol
press fx
release fx
release vol
midicc 1 23 64
snap learn_follow_bound
assert state overlay=LEARN keys=~F5:FOLLOW
tap f5
snap learn_follow
assert state overlay=LEARN keys=~F5:FOLLOW msg=~FOLLOWS
tap f5
snap learn_follow_pinned
assert state overlay=LEARN keys=~F5:FOLLOW msg=~PINNED
press vol
press fx
release fx
release vol
