# OS-LIVEREC, OS-RECMODE2: a live recording pass on the Pattern screen (the maintainer's design; new Community OS behaviour). The demo document; the sequencer's position is the
# scenario's `playhead <step> [<ustep>]` (what the engine publishes). In LIVE notes go to the PLAYHEAD's step on the CURSOR's track, the selected instrument; REC QUANTIZE
# is Nearest step by default (a note played after half a step lands on the next one). STEP (Rec) writes at the cursor.

document
snap stopped
tap rec
snap step_stopped
tap rec

# Playing, Shift + Rec is LIVE (OS-RECMODE2; a plain Rec is STEP): the cursor rides the playhead and the view keeps the playing row centred (OS-CENTER). Right / Left change the recording track
playhead 3
tap rec
press shift
tap rec
release shift
snap follow_3
tap right
snap follow_track_2

# A pad hit in step 3: the note is written at the cursor, which is the playhead's step; the release in the same step puts the OFF in the next one.
pad 14
snap note_recorded

# Late in step 9 (30 of its 48 microsteps played): Nearest step puts the note on step 10, over what the demo had there.
playhead 9 30
pad 17
snap nearest_next_step

# Shift + Rec in LIVE goes back to where LIVE began, here STEP (OS-LIVEEXIT, OS-UXF-PAT item 8; from WATCH it goes back to WATCH): the take ends (one undo unit), the cursor and the view stay, the user moves on their own; the note goes to the cursor, not to the playhead.
press shift
tap rec
release shift
playhead 14
snap step_playhead_14
tap down 2
tap left
playhead 15
snap step_cursor_moved
pad 19
snap step_note_at_cursor

# Shift + Rec is LIVE again, a new take: the cursor is on the playhead at once. Rec in LIVE is WATCH (OS-LIVEEXIT), which follows too but records nothing.
press shift
tap rec
release shift
snap live_again
tap rec
snap watch_again
playhead stop
snap stopped_again

# The step entry is one undo unit, and the whole take, every note and OFF from the first hit to the Rec, is ONE undo unit: two Undos take the pass back.
action edit.undo
action edit.undo
snap undone
