# OS-E2E J2: the "OG project migration" journey. A project made by the original firmware's folder layout (the card e2e_og: Projects/OG Demo/{project.mt, patterns/pattern_01.mtp, instruments/01 Sine.pti}, one sample
# instrument, a loop of 200 frames = two periods of a 441 Hz sine, and sixteen steps of 75 ms at 200 BPM) is opened from the card, played, edited, saved as a native project, exported back to the OG layout, the
# exported folder is opened again (the user copies it from Export/ into Projects/ on a computer: `card copy`), saved native and reloaded, and heard again. About 1.8 s of audio and 7 s a run under the sanitizers, against the spec's target of under 6 s (the live take 0.7 s, the reloaded
# project 0.7 s, the export read back 0.4 s). The card is GENERATED, never committed: target e2e_og_fixture runs `o3og_fixture` (tests/tools/o3og_fixture.cpp, from the synthetic byte maps of tests/support/og_fixture.h,
# docs/formats/og.md; nothing of the official firmware); ctest e2e_fixture_fresh holds it to what the tool writes. Evidence label: new Community OS behaviour (test only, no firmware file is touched). The OG leg is
# checked by field probes, not by bytes or by a digest: an OG export resamples and converts levels by design (tests/og_roundtrip_test.cpp). The takes are compared by measurement, not by a sample null: the first block of
# a take falls at another phase of the engine's pacing (up to 1.45 ms, docs/specs/OS-E2E.md, E2E-13).
# Known cases (docs/specs/OS-E2E.md, J2, "Found, not changed"): (a) the project name of the OG export is the model's ("OG Demo"), not the folder the project was saved to ("Track 01"); the probe `name=OG Demo`
# of step 5 pins that until the maintainer decides which name an export should carry. (b) F1 on the EXPORT page acts on the row the cursor was left on (OG PROJECT from an earlier visit), so the journey moves
# the cursor first (`tap down 4`, `tap up 4`).
document
wait 20
card e2e_og
audio on
wait 20

# 1. File > OPEN of an OG folder (the listing tags it as OG by its project.mt): the OG loader reads the project, its pattern and the .pti, the sample loader binds the PCM. Probes of what the files said.

# 2. Play the pattern and capture it (the tracks too): C-5, G-5, C-5 on steps 1, 3, 5 (75 ms a step: 75, 225 and 375 ms), a note off on step 7 (525 ms). The sample is a loop of a sine of 12000/32768 (a peak of -8.73 dB and
# an RMS of -11.74 dB at unity); the instrument's volume 40 and panning 60 (not the defaults, so that the probes after the re-import can catch a dropped field) take 4.80 dB off in the engine: peak -13.53 dB and
# RMS -16.54 dB as measured. A note plays at the sample's pitch at C-5 (441.18 Hz as measured), 661 Hz at G-5.
render live 700 stems
tap play
wait 700
tap play
wait 1
assert state playing=0
assert audio live.t1 in=100-220 pitch=441.18+-4c rms=-16.54+-0.3 peak=-13.53+-0.3 clicks=0
assert audio live.t1 in=250-370 pitch=661.00+-4c
assert audio live.t1 in=400-520 pitch=441.18+-4c
assert audio live.t1 in=0-60 silent
assert audio live.t1 in=560-690 silent
assert audio live.t1 in=0-700 onset=75+-3 clicks=0
assert audio live.t2 silent
assert audio live in=100-220 pitch=441.18+-4c

# The OG leg, by field probes: the export wrote what the model held (the name of the project, the tempo, the song, the notes with the edit, the note off, the instrument and the fields of its sampler), and
# not byte for byte or by digest: an OG export resamples, converts levels and normalises by design (tests/og_roundtrip_test.cpp), so only fields the format carries are compared.
# The name pins known case (a) of the header: the export carries the model's name, not the folder's (Track 01). If a later change gives the export the folder's name, this line is the one to move.
