# OS-E2E-13: the "pro session" journey. A power user's session on the card of a simulator: open a kit, start a NEW project that keeps its instruments, write a song of five tracks and three patterns by the keys, play
# it, mute and solo, hold a Perform slot and RESET it, export the song as a WAV, SAVE AS, edit, reload the saved project, and play the song again. One scenario (one card: the scratch copy of the card is removed when
# a run ends, so the steps cannot be chained across files); about 2.7 s of audio in five windows (song 1.0 s, mix 0.3, perf 0.42, again 1.0, and the export read back), 10 s a run under the sanitizers.
# The card e2e_pro is GENERATED (target e2e_pro_fixture: `o3pj_tool gen kit`, tests/tools/o3pj_tool.cpp; tests/e2e_pro_fixture_test.py holds it to what the generator writes): Projects/Kit/project.o3pj with four
# sample instruments (1 SINE, a loop of 100 frames a period at 44,100 Hz: 441.18 Hz at C-5; 2 SAW on the AMIGA engine, 32 frames a period at the Paula rate 8,287 Hz; 3 PLUCK, a one shot that decays by e every 0.12 s;
# 4 HAT, a 50 ms noise burst) and the MIDI instrument 49 SYNTH, and their WAVs in samples/. No amplitude envelope, so a note is the sample. Every pitch is the sample's own at the pad's semitone.
# The song (400 BPM: a step is 37.5 ms; the Tempo FX 15 at value 100 is 200 BPM): pattern 1 has four steps that DUPLICATE x2 made eight (T1 C G F G, T2 E G, T3 C and C an octave up by the Tune FX, T4 a burst on every
# second step by the Fill dialog, T5 the MIDI track); pattern 2 is its copy (F1 held, Copy, Shift + Copy) with a new bass line; pattern 3 is another copy SHRUNK /2 to four steps with the Tempo FX on T4's first step.
# Evidence label: new Community OS behaviour (test only; no firmware file is touched). The master is not compared sample for sample with a soloed stem (docs/specs/OS-E2E.md, E2E-13, finding F1), and the
# second take is compared by measurement, not by a null, because its first block falls at another phase of the engine's pacing (up to 1.45 ms).
document
wait 20
card e2e_pro
audio on
wait 20
