snap song_page1

# Quarter jumps in the Song screen: Shift + F4 is three quarters through the 24 positions, Shift + F5 the last (OS-SHIFTROW: the slot with a copy of the pattern is Shift + Insert).

release shift
snap song_three_quarters
press shift
tap f5
release shift
snap song_end

# ... and back to Pattern. Browse is the default mode (the chip says WATCH); Rec enters STEP (OS-RECMODE2: the chip says STEP), the cursor is left alone and the arrows move it

tap rec

tap down
snap pattern_rec_step_page
tap rec

press shift

release shift

# The pads' mode keys (OS-PADS-A, OS-PADOCT): Shift held shows the picker on the key row (F1 says the octave, "C3"; the mode that stays inverted); Shift + F5 leaves TRACKS fixed
# (the info line says it), Shift + F2 puts NOTES back. They do not jump: the quarter jumps (Shift + F1..F5) are the Song list's and the Navigator's now.
# (Shift + F1 moves the octave up: the last block of this file, so that the octave stays C3 for the shots between.)
press shift
wait 20
snap pattern_picker

snap pattern_picker_tracks
release shift
snap pattern_pads_tracks
press shift

release shift

# The Navigator (OS-NAV2): a tap of Shift + Pattern opens the view grid and it STAYS open after the keys are let go (a long wait changes nothing);
# the arrow chooses Song, F8 is GO (the footer says F7 CLOSE / F8 GO, and the screen's F-key row under it is the Navigator's: F1 GO, F7 CLOSE, F8 GO, and
# the quarter jumps while Shift is still held).
press shift
tap pattern
snap navigator_open_shift
release shift
wait 450
snap navigator_open
assert state overlay=NAVIGATOR keys=~F8:GO
tap right
snap navigator_song
tap f8
snap navigator_opened_song
assert state screen=SONG overlay=NONE

tap pattern
press shift
press pattern

release pattern
release shift
snap navigator_held_open
encoder 1
tap down
snap navigator_down
tap f7
snap navigator_closed
