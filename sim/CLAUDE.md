# sim/ — headless simulator `o3sim`, scenarios, gallery, goldens

- Owns the host simulator: scripted input, a `FakeClock` that moves 1 ms per tick (a run is a pure function of the script: no wall clock), the integer-only `EveRaster` interpreter of FT81x display-list words, the scenarios, the gallery (screen x state x theme) and the SHA-256 goldens. Goldens are simulator truth, not device truth. Must not: use wall time or floating point in the raster. No other layer may include `sim/` (it may include all of them).
- Entry points:
  - `README.md`: scenario grammar, fidelity table, gallery, calibration (read this first)
  - `src/main.cpp`: CLI (`--scenario`, `--gallery`, `--legibility`, `--goldens`, `--check`, `--twice`, `--theme`, `--cards`, `--wav`, `--fingerprints`, `--card-rate`, `--card-latency`, `--card-write-rate`)
  - `src/scenario.cpp`, `include/o3os/sim/scenario.h`: the parser; `src/simulator.cpp`: the loop
  - `src/sim_project.cpp`, `include/o3os/sim/sim_project.h`, `Simulator::open_card` (`src/simulator.cpp`) and `platform/host/dir_card.h`: the card of the scenario command `card <fixture-dir>` (OS-E2E-1b): a scratch copy of the fixture (`e2e_card`, GENERATED into `<build>/sim/cards/` by the target `e2e_card_fixture`: `o3pj_tool gen small`, never committed; `o3sim --cards <dir>` finds it, ctest and `update_goldens` pass it, and a by-hand run of `scenarios/e2e/e2e_card.txt` needs `--cards build-os-host/sim/cards`), a write side behind the real path guard, the sample memory and bank, and the project loader behind File > OPEN, NEW and, since OS-E2E-9, IMPORT of a module (`SimProject::import_module`; a copy of the platform's `finish_load` and `start_module_import` sequences: they live in the composition root, which is firmware, so they cannot be shared); `assert file` reads it
  - `src/scenario_assert.cpp`, `include/o3os/sim/scenario_assert.h`: `assert state|model|file` and `mark model` (OS-E2E-1a): `diag state` pairs (`~regex`), model paths, the native-file digest, files of the card; read only, no tick (the goldens do not move when a scenario gains one); the grammar is at the top of the header
  - `src/sim_ptfx.cpp`, `Simulator::card_ptfx` (OS-CEPTFX S4a): the scenario line `card ptfx` writes seven synthetic header-only plugins into the card folder's `Effects/` for the PLUGINS page of Sample edit (made in code, never run); `Simulator::firmware_on` (`src/simulator.cpp`), scenario command `firmware on` (OS-FWCONFIG): the fake update port (`platform/host/fake_firmware.h`) behind Config INFO's F8 UPDATE, four scripted files (`TRACKER_1.PTF` good, `SLOW_CHECK.PTF` a 600-pass check, `BAD_IMAGE.PTF` a failed check, `NEVER_ENDS.PTF` a hand-over that never ends); the walk is `scenarios/qa/qa_config_update.txt` (+ `_refused`), the states are gallery `config_fw_*` and `extreme_firmware`; no flash, no card (`README.md`)
  - `src/sim_audio.cpp`, `include/o3os/sim/sim_audio.h`, `Simulator::audio_on` (OS-E2E-2): the scenario command `audio on` puts the REAL engine behind the app (`audio::Engine`, the sampler voices, the sequencer as its event source, the send effects, the master limiter, the app's transport, preview and mix sinks: a copy of the unit's wiring in `platform/mk66/src/app_main.cpp`, which is firmware and cannot be shared), paced by an integer accumulator (0 or 1 block of 64 frames a 1 ms tick at 44,117.647 Hz: no wall clock), with a test bank bound through `app::make_sample_desc` when there is no card (sine, noise and a pluck, built by integer arithmetic: no file, no libm; the header has the table). `render <name> <ms> [stems]` captures the master (and the stem tap's T1..T8 and master pair) for a window of simulated time; `o3sim --wav <dir>` writes the renders for listening; `--twice` compares their SHA-256. `document sound` is the document of the audio journeys (`demo_project.h`: an empty pattern at 120 BPM and four neutral instruments). `pad <n> hold|down|up` and `encoder <n> x<k> every <ms>` hold a note and sweep a knob while it sounds. A project load with the engine on stops the transport and waits for the voices (`SimProject::attach_sound`). Since OS-E2E-9 it also has the Amiga voices (with the Tick events), the WAV export (`OfflineRender`/`ExportSource` into the save layer; the live callback is the engine's own `trampoline`) and the TB-303 module (the host shared object bound to a Module instrument that names it; `document tb303`); `wav <name> <card path>` reads an exported WAV back as a render. Not simulated: the SD modules but the TB-303 (the shipped apps are `apps on`, below), the MIDI link, the recorder (the grammar is in `README.md`, "Audio")
  - `src/sim_apps.cpp`, `include/o3os/sim/sim_apps.h`, `Simulator::apps_on` (OS-APPUX S0): the scenario command `apps on` gives the card the shipped apps: their host shared objects (`o3m_<name>_host`, the files the per-app tests dlopen) are registered as native modules (`ModuleSet::add_native`, with the capability word of the manifest's generated header), `modules/apps/<stem>.tmod` placeholders are written to the card's scratch copy, and the app gets `app::ModuleUi` over the card (the simulator's clock, an idle audio tap, a MIDI-in ring nothing feeds). The list is `O3SIM_APPS` in `sim/CMakeLists.txt` (the configure fails when it differs from the `module.json` files of `modules/apps`) and the rig's table is GENERATED from it (`<build>/sim/gen/sim_apps_table.h`). Gives the app pages their goldens: `scenarios/qa/qa_modules.txt`. Refused with `audio on`; `tests/sim_apps_test.cpp`. Not simulated: the loader's checks of a real file, the MIDI link, the engine behind the tap
  - `src/audio_check.cpp`, `include/o3os/sim/audio_check.h`: `assert audio <name> [in=<from>-<to>] <check> ...` (OS-E2E-2): pure functions over a render (`double` is fine here): pitch (YIN, refined on the spectrum, 50 Hz to 5 kHz), rms, peak, DC, silence, clicks (the second difference against its local spread), onset, the ADSR level curve, centroid, pan, zipper (spectral-flux spikes), alias (Blackman-Harris), null (with sums of renders and a lag), and the grammar; every tolerance and its reason is at the top of the header, `tests/audio_check_test.cpp` has each on synthetic sines, impulses, steps, sweeps and planted clicks. A measurement has a tolerance and never a sample hash: the floats are reproducible on one machine and compiler, not across them
  - `src/audio_fingerprint.cpp`, `include/o3os/sim/audio_fingerprint.h` (OS-E2E-4): the scenario line `fingerprint <render> [nof0]` and `o3sim --fingerprints <dir> (--check-fingerprints | --update-fingerprints) [--fingerprint-diff <file>]`: the compact description of a render (24 band levels, f0, peak, RMS, balance, DC, clicks by 100 ms frame on 200 ms Hann windows, the onsets) in `tests/audio_fingerprints/<scenario>.json`, the comparison rules (spectral distance max 1.5 / mean 0.5 dB, onsets 1 ms, f0 10 cents, plus levels, DC and clicks), the report. `scenario_*` ctests check it, `cmake --build <dir> --target update_audio_fingerprints` (`tools/update_audio_fingerprints.py`) rewrites the files and writes `<dir>/sim/fingerprint/fingerprint_diff.md` and the WAVs; `tools/check_fingerprint_guard.py` (ctest `fingerprint_guard`) wants a firmware source directory (`app/`, `audio/`, `core/`, `model/`, `seq/`, `formats/`, `modules/`, `module/`), the scenario or the rig in the same commit as a changed file, with a body of two lines at least. `tests/audio_fingerprint_test.cpp` (unit_audio)
  - `src/eve_raster.cpp`: rasteriser (with the bitmap log of the legibility sweep, off unless `record_glyphs`); `src/gallery.cpp` + `src/screens*.cpp`: the states of every screen (`screens_legibility.cpp`: the widest text the model's limits allow); `src/demo_project.cpp`
  - `src/legibility.cpp`, `include/o3os/sim/legibility.h`: `o3sim --legibility`, the readability check of every gallery picture (OS-E2E-5): ink cut by the scissor or the screen edge, text on text, a colour pair outside the theme's contrast table; the named allow list is at the top of the .cpp
- Random user (OS-QANET B1, `docs/specs/OS-QANET.md` part B): `tests/qanet/random_user.cpp` (program `o3_qanet_random_user`, not doctest) drives a `Simulator` with a seeded PRNG over every screen on the large card `qanet_card` (about 2000 files in 50 folders, GENERATED by `tools/qanet_card.py` into `<build>/sim/cards/` by the target `qanet_card_fixture`, never committed) and checks invariants after every step (display list, listing empty against a scan of our own, NO SD CARD with the card in, job bounds, open directories at rest, undo digest after a burst). Findings are keyed `invariant | screen | action` in `tests/qanet/baseline_random.txt` (seed@step is the repro text only). ctest `qanet_random_user_selftest` and `qanet_random_user_short` (labels `app`, `qanet`; in `ci.sh host`), `qanet_random_user_<i>of8` (label `e2e`: the 200 seeds; `O3OS_QANET_SEED`, `O3OS_QANET_SEEDS` and `O3OS_QANET_STEPS` set the range, `tools/remote_ci.sh` passes them to the remote machine: `O3OS_QANET_SEEDS=2000 tools/remote_ci.sh e2e`). By hand: `o3_qanet_random_user --card build-os-host/sim/cards/qanet_card --seed 1 --seeds 20 --verbose`. A tick costs about 5 ms under the sanitizers, so a seed of 300 steps takes about 17 s.
- FatFs card (OS-QANET D1): a `card` fixture that is a FILE is a FAT32 image mounted through the unit's own FatFs (`platform/host/fat/`: `FatFsStorage` = `FatStorage` + `FatStorageWriter` over `ImageDevice`, a private mmap of the image; `FatImage` writes a folder into an image; `Simulator::open_card` picks by what the fixture is; `HostCard` is what the simulator holds a card by, `DirCard` the other). `tests/tools/o3fatimg.cpp <folder> <image> [--size-mb N]` makes an image (target `card_images`: `<build>/sim/cards_fat/<card>.img` for every card of `sim/cards/`); ctest `scenario_fatfs_<journey>` (label `e2e`) runs the `e2e/` journeys that use a card on it (`o3sim --cards <build>/sim/cards_fat`), `tests/fat_fs_storage_test.cpp` (`fat image:*`, unit_rest) holds the image, the guard and the pull; `scenarios/e2e/e2e_newlist.txt` is OS-BUG-NEWLIST's journey (File, NEW, Sample load, the listing asserted) on the folder and on the image. Differences from the unit: the device is memory (no SDHC timing, D2), one-sector clusters (a 64 MiB FAT32 needs them; the card has 4 to 32 KB), the stamp of every entry 2024-11-01 as the unit's NORTC; the file system code and its configuration are the unit's.
- Slow card (OS-QANET D2): `platform/host/slow_card.h` (`SlowCard`, a `HostCard` over any card; `CardTiming`, the switch and the numbers) charges latency plus bytes over the rate to the simulated clock in the pass where the app's call happens, and `Simulator::card_time` lets the audio render through the stretched pass (one block per millisecond of the clock). The app, the project loader, the apps and the screenshot see the wrapper; `assert file`, `card_storage()` and the slot's switch use the card itself (no time). Scenario `card slow <bytes-per-ms> <latency-us> [<write-bytes-per-ms>]` / `card slow off`, options `--card-rate`, `--card-latency`, `--card-write-rate`; defaults 17700 bytes/ms, 257 us, 442 bytes/ms write (PROVISIONAL: a fit to the unit's block bench, OS-USBDISK 4a, until D4). Off, nothing changes. Card full (OS-QANET B8): `CardTiming::full` (independent of `on`) makes the wrapper refuse every write that would grow the card (`make_dir`, `create`, `create_marker`, any written byte) with `WriteStatus::NoSpace`; reads, renames and deletes work. Scenario `card full` / `card full off`, `Simulator::set_card_full`; the journey is `scenarios/e2e/e2e_card_full.txt`. `tests/slow_card_test.cpp` (`slow card:*`, unit_rest). `Simulator::uptime_us()` and `card_charged_us()` read the clock and the charge.
- Pro session (OS-E2E-13): `scenarios/e2e/e2e_pro_session.txt`, one long journey (a kit card in, NEW keeping its instruments, five tracks by the keys, PAT-OPS, Song with a tempo change, mute/solo, Perform hold + RESET, EXPORT, SAVE AS, reload, the song again); its card `e2e_pro` is GENERATED by `o3pj_tool gen kit` (target `e2e_pro_fixture`, test `e2e_pro_fixture_fresh`); the unit twin is `platform/mk66/tools/hil/pro_session.json`. Findings: docs/specs/OS-E2E.md "E2E-13"; `scenarios/e2e/e2e_new_after_save.txt` checks its F2, fixed by OS-BUG-PROMPTQ (a question takes the strip's head at once, ahead of the SAVE AS message: `NotifyQueue::ask`).
- Perform latch (OS-PERFLATCH): `scenarios/e2e/e2e_perform_latch.txt`, a looped noise note on T1 with a LOWPASS slot held by pad 15: the filter ends with the pad when the encoder's value is only the live one (`PerformBank::pad_up` reloads the stored value, which the scenario first sets to 0 because a new project holds the OS-PRFDEFAULTS default -30: by design, pinned) and stays latched when `Rec` (EDIT) stored it, until row 0 (QA-PRF-025); one render of 1.27 s, measured by the noise's centroid.
- Reference players (OS-E2E-9): `scenarios/e2e/e2e_ref_*.txt` journeys whose renders `tests/e2e_openmpt_ref.py` (libopenmpt), `tests/e2e_dsp_ref.py` (`tools/dsp_ref.py`, scipy) and `tests/e2e_open303_ref.py` (the original Open303, `tests/tools/o3ref303_tool.cpp`) compare with a reference; the modules they import are GENERATED by `tools/e2e_ref_cards.py` into `<build>/sim/cards/` (target `e2e_ref_fixture`), never committed. ctest `e2e_ref_*` (label `e2e`; 19 `_break_*` tests perturb the reference and must fail, `_break_near_*` at about twice the tolerance; exit 77 without libopenmpt 0.7.x, numpy or scipy, a failure under `O3_CI=1`; `e2e_ref_lib_selftest` checks the shared helpers); `scenario_e2e_ref_*` run each journey twice. Tolerances and findings: docs/specs/OS-E2E.md "E2E-9".
- Add a scenario: `scenarios/<name>.txt`, `scenarios/qa/qa_<screen>.txt`, `scenarios/e2e/e2e_<name>.txt` or `scenarios/manual/<screen>_walk.txt` (a manual chapter's walk-through, its snaps are the chapter's pictures: `docs/manual/images.txt`, `<screen>_walk_<step>.png manual/<screen>_walk walk_<step>`) (one command per line, `snap <name>` captures, `assert ...` checks; a journey with sound is `e2e_audio*.txt`: `document sound`, `audio on`, `render`, `assert audio`, no `snap`, so no golden, and under 2 s of audio; the E2E-13 pro session is the one exception, about 3 s in five windows). Every `scenarios/*.txt` and `scenarios/<folder>/*.txt` is globbed at configure time and becomes ctest `scenario_<file name>`; the name is also the goldens prefix, so a file carries its folder's prefix or suffix (`qa_`, `e2e_`, `_walk`) and two of one name stop the configure and `update_goldens`. Then regenerate the goldens (a scenario with no `snap` has none). `e2e_*` scenarios have the ctest label `e2e` (`ci.sh host` leaves them out, `ci.sh e2e` runs them). A new screen state goes in `src/screens*.cpp`.
- Regenerate goldens (never edit or merge `goldens.txt` by hand; on a merge conflict regenerate): `cmake --build build-os-host --target update_goldens` (runs `tools/update_goldens.py`, parallel up to `O3OS_GOLDEN_JOBS`, default 4). `O3OS_GOLDEN_ONLY=<name>` refreshes one scenario and/or the gallery screens named `<name>*`. Then LOOK at `build-os-host/sim/png/*/*.png` and `build-os-host/sim/gallery/*.png`; these are not committed. The manual's screenshots are (`docs/manual/img/`, list in `docs/manual/images.txt`): after a change that moves a screen, run `python3 tools/manual_images.py`, commit the PNGs and re-read the chapters that show them (ctest `o3sim_manual_images` fails on a stale one).
- One scenario by hand: `build-os-host/sim/o3sim --scenario sim/scenarios/<name>.txt --goldens sim/goldens.txt --check --twice`.
- Tests: ctest `scenario_*`, `gallery_<i>of6`, `gallery_shards_cover`, `o3sim_*` exit-code tests, `o3sim_manual_images`, `update_goldens_selftest`, `scenario_e2e_*` (label `e2e`, like `legibility_*`), `o3sim_assert_*` and `o3sim_card_*` (a failed or mistaken assertion or card command is exit 2: `negative/assert_*.txt`, `negative/card_*.txt`), the cases of `tests/scenario_test.cpp` (`assert`, `mark`), `tests/sim_card_test.cpp` (the card in the simulator: open, save, pull, an OG project, NEW) and `tests/dir_card_test.cpp` (`DirCard`: the guard, a rename that never replaces, a pulled card), `e2e_card_fixture_fresh` (the generated card is what `o3pj_tool gen small` writes, and nothing else), `sim_project_drift` (labels `static,platform`: every call of the platform's `finish_load`, `begin_samples` and `begin_replace` is in `sim_project.cpp` or on the named "not simulated" list of `tests/sim_project_drift_test.py`), the cases of `tests/audio_check_test.cpp` (the measurements, unit_audio) and `tests/sim_audio_test.cpp` (the engine in the simulator: the commands, the pacing, a render, the same samples twice, the sinks, a load while playing, every refusal), `o3sim_audio_*_exit2` (`negative/audio_*.txt`: a failed measurement or a mistaken audio command, `wav` included, is exit 2), `scenario_e2e_audio*` (the journeys: label `e2e`, `ci.sh e2e` copies their WAVs to `build-e2e/<date>/wav/`), `legibility_<i>of6` (label `e2e`: only `tools/ci.sh e2e` runs them; `ci.sh host` leaves them out; `o3sim_legibility_mixer_exit0` runs the Mixer's 51 pictures in `ci.sh host`, 2 s), `e2e_report_selftest`, `qa_cover` and `qa_cover_selftest` (the QA catalogue gate, `tools/qa_cover.py`: every row's Sim snap, gallery picture, HIL step and host test must exist or the row says `manual: <reason>`; `python3 -I tools/qa_cover.py --write` regenerates the README index; forms in `docs/qa/catalogue/README.md`). A finding of `legibility_*` names the picture (`gallery/<screen>_<state>_<theme>`) and the glyph: look at that PNG (`o3sim --gallery <dir> --only <screen> --theme <name>`), fix the app or, for a deliberate overlap, add a named entry to the allow list. Scoped: `ctest --test-dir build-os-host -R 'scenario_|gallery_' --output-on-failure` (or `-L app -LE e2e`; `-L e2e` for the legibility sweep and the journeys); `O3OS_CI_SCOPE=changed tools/ci.sh host` maps `sim/` to the `app` label.
- Docs: `sim/README.md`, `docs/specs/OS-001-walking-skeleton.md`, ADR 0010. QA scenarios are `sim/scenarios/qa/qa_<screen>.txt`; the catalogue row's Sim cell is `qa/qa_<screen>.txt › <snap>` (row format in `app/CLAUDE.md`). `o3hil` can compare a unit screenshot with the sim picture (`--sim build-os-host/sim/o3sim`; steps in `platform/mk66/tools/hil/`).
