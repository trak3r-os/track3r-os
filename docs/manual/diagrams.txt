# The format, and what `--check` fails on, is in the docstring of tools/manual_diagrams.py. The keys of a chord diagram are not written here:
# each `bind` line names a row of app/src/bindings.cpp (context, action, argument) and the diagram takes the keys from it, so a diagram cannot
# drift from the firmware. The boot chords come from platform/mk66/src/app_main.cpp, the Power hold from core/power_hold.h.
# Each stanza says the chapter it goes in; --check fails unless that chapter shows it with this alt text.
# A figure is a small crop of the panel (`region`), marks at most four keys (MAX_KEYS in the script; the keys that share a label, like the four arrows of a
# block, count once), and a chapter keeps one chord figure plus those that say `extra = <why>`. A figure of a Shift row (Shift + F1..F8 only) needs
# `why = <...>`: the unit labels its Shift row itself, and panel.md says once that Shift relabels F1 to F8.

# ---- The signal flow -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Drawn from the engine's graph, not generated from a table: FLOW_CODE in tools/manual_diagrams.py names, for every box, a symbol of one of the files
# below, and --check fails when one is gone (or when this list and the boxes' files differ). What the boxes say, and where it is written:
#   the order of one block: engine.h (the header comment) and graph.h (the header comment: strip pass, buses, master chain, USB return, monitoring)
#   a track's strip: graph.cpp finish_track (CUE and REC taps, the meter, the stem tap after the level), graph_params.h route:: (the route bits)
#   the sends and their returns: graph.h SendFxFn, graph_params.h RetRouteA / RetRouteB (MAIN and CUE)
#   the master chain: graph.h MasterStage::Limiter (ALWAYS ON); app_main.cpp installs the limiter and no other master stage (checked)
#   the outputs: graph.cpp run_levels (OUT MODE: graph_params.h OutMode::MainLCueR), the USB return added there after the master level
#   USB capture: taps.h StemTap (post-fader tracks with the route bit, master pair, the two sends); the click and the loop: graph.cpp mix_extras
