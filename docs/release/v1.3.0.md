# track3r-os v1.3.0: release notes

v1.3.0 adds MIDI recording into lanes, automation of any learnable parameter from the pattern, a LEARN MAP screen, a STATUS LED BPM mode, an advanced cheat sheet, and the four instrument synths as card modules. The full list is in the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md).

track3r-os is community firmware for the Polyend Tracker. It is unofficial and not affiliated with Polyend. Installing it replaces the firmware in your unit. It is licensed GPL-3.0-or-later. Third-party licences are in [THIRD_PARTY.md](../../THIRD_PARTY.md).

**Back up your SD card and keep the official firmware file before you install.**

## Read this first

- **Copy the new modules zip to the card.** TB-303, Chip, FM and SID load from `modules/instruments/`. Without them those instruments are muted and say `MODULE ... NOT LOADED`. Overwrite every older copy in `modules/instruments/`.
- **Going back.** v1.3.0 images are padded to a whole 4 KiB flash sector and end below 0xFF000, as v1.2.0 images do. Going back to an older file works as described in the [v1.2.0 notes](v1.2.0.md).
- **Module import needs a file.** MOD, S3M and MED import runs from `modules/importers/tracker.tmod`, which is in the content zip. Without it IMPORT refuses and your open project is untouched.
- **Project files.** A project with MIDI lanes is saved with the `MEVT` chunk, which older firmware keeps as an unknown chunk. A project with CC letters is saved as INST 1.9; an older firmware says "newer fields skipped" but nothing is lost.

## What is new

- **MIDI recording into lanes**: notes and chords, sub-step timing and length, external CC, a Learn knob and the encoder as a CC letter, REC STORE, the EVENTS list over the lanes, and one undo per take.
- **Automation of learnable parameters** from the pattern, with up to six CC letters per project.
- **LEARN MAP** (`F6` MAP in the Learn dialog, or `F8` on Config > MIDI).
- **STATUS LED BPM mode** on Config.
- **Advanced cheat sheet**: [manual/advanced.md](../manual/advanced.md).

## What is fixed

- **Master + Insert** opens the Mixer's INSERTS page (issue #5).
- **Shift + pad** plays the instrument instead of selecting it (issue #9).
- **Slice pads** play their own slice (issue #7).
- **Quieter power-off** (issue #8).

## Files

- `track3r-os-1.3.0.ptf`, the update file.
- `track3r-os-1.3.0.zip`, the starter content: the modules and the examples in one zip. Unzip at the card root.
- `track3r-os-v1.3.0-modules.zip`, every external module, the factory presets and the `.ptfx` Sample Editor plugins. Unzip at the card root.
- `track3r-os-v1.3.0-examples.zip`, demo projects, a drum kit, wavetables, an OctaMED module and the licences of the files. Unzip at the card root.
- `SHA256SUMS`, the checksums of the files.

Check your download with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`. The layout of the zips is in `tools/release/README.md`.

**Module ABI.** It is 1.13, as in v1.2.0. Old `.tmod` files of ABI 1.0 to 1.10 still load, as in v1.2.0. A module built for a newer minor than the OS is refused with NEEDS NEWER OS.

## Install or update

- **From track3r-os v1.2.0, v1.1.0, v1.0.1 or v1.0.0 (with working pads):** copy `track3r-os-1.3.0.ptf` into `Firmware/`, unzip the two content zips at the card root, put the card in the unit, open Config, INFO and press `F8` (UPDATE).
- **From the official firmware, or when the pads are dead:** copy everything on the card to your computer; copy `track3r-os-1.3.0.ptf` into `Firmware/` and rename the copy to `PolyendTracker_0.0.1.ptf` (it must be the only `.ptf` there); use the hidden reset button, as in Polyend's [emergency flashing page](https://polyend.ladesk.com/929798-How-do-I-perform-the-emergency-flashing-procedure); after the restart, open Config, INFO. BUILD ID should show `v1.3.0`. Then unzip the content zips at the card root.

The full steps are in the [installing guide](../manual/install.md).

## Tested on the unit

On an original Tracker, release candidate b13, over USB MIDI and USB audio:

- MIDI recording into lanes: single notes, 3-note chords, notes between steps and notes longer than one step, in LIVE and in STEP. EVENTS shows the lane rows with their ticks and lengths, and playback sends the same notes back with as many note-offs as note-ons.
- CC recording: an external CC and a Learn-bound CC on a CC letter go into the lane and play back. One undo takes a whole take back (notes and CCs). REC STORE = Grid records into the pattern as before.
- A project with lanes keeps them after saving and a power cycle. A stop during the count-in leaves no stray note.
- Five minutes of recording with MIDI in: CPU max 58.6 %, no underrun, no late or dropped block.
- CC FX on 8 tracks (parameter automation): CPU max 82.5 %, no underrun.
- Not yet checked on the unit: a relative Learn knob, and Chip's new waves with the 1.3.0 module.

## Not yet verified
