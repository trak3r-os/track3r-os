# track3r-os v1.0.1: release notes

v1.0.1 fixes the pads on later Trackers. Everything else is v1.0.0. The full list of changes is in the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md); the v1.0.0 notes are in [v1.0.0.md](v1.0.0.md).

track3r-os is community firmware for the Polyend Tracker. It is unofficial and not affiliated with Polyend. Installing it replaces the firmware in your unit. It is licensed GPL-3.0-or-later. Third-party licences are in [THIRD_PARTY.md](../../THIRD_PARTY.md).

**Back up your SD card and keep the official firmware file before you install.**

## What is fixed

- **The pads did not answer on later Trackers** (plastic back, boards Control 4.0 / Grid 2.0; issue #1). The unit started, the buttons and the screen worked, the status LED flashed 13 times at power-on and the pad grid stayed dark and dead. The pad board is now started later and more gently, and its chips are asked again until they answer. A user with an affected unit confirmed that the pads and note entry work. On first-batch units (metal back) nothing changes.

## Files

- `track3r-os-v1.0.1.ptf`, the update file. For a first install from the official firmware you rename your copy on the card (see Install).
- `SHA256SUMS`, the checksum of the `.ptf`.

Check your download with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.

The app modules are the same as for v1.0.0: the release `apps-v1.0.0` on the Releases page.

## Install or update

- **From track3r-os v1.0.0:** copy `track3r-os-v1.0.1.ptf` into `Firmware/` on the card, put the card in the unit, open Config, INFO and press `F8` (UPDATE). Or install it as below.
- **From the official firmware, or when the pads are dead (v1.0.0 on a later unit):**
  1. Copy everything on the SD card to your computer.
  2. Copy `track3r-os-v1.0.1.ptf` into `Firmware/` and rename the copy to `PolyendTracker_0.0.1.ptf`. It must be the only `.ptf` there. Do not use the Polyend Tool app.
  3. Use the hidden reset button, as in Polyend's [emergency flashing page](https://polyend.ladesk.com/929798-How-do-I-perform-the-emergency-flashing-procedure).
  4. After the restart, open Config, INFO. BUILD ID should show `v1.0.1`.

The full steps and the way back to the official firmware are in the [installing guide](../manual/install.md).

## Known issues

The known issues of v1.0.0 stay, except the pads:

- Swapping the SD card can crash the unit. Switch the unit off before you change the card.
- Very heavy projects can exceed the CPU budget; some Perform effects cost CPU while held. See the [CPU budget](../architecture/cpu-budget.md).
- The update list on the unit shows only the first 12 files of `Firmware/`. Keep 12 or fewer files there.

## Not yet verified

- The pads on other later board revisions: one unit has been reported working.
- The items listed as not yet verified in the [v1.0.0 notes](v1.0.0.md).
