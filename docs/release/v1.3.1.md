# track3r-os v1.3.1: release notes

v1.3.1 tidies the keys: `Shift`+`File` saves from any screen, `F1` accepts and `F8` goes back on every overlay, Focus Loop loops a selected block, one `Shift`+`F2` PADS picker replaces five pad chords, CHOP writes its slices into the pattern, and the encoder assign moves into the Learn dialog. The full list is in the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md).

track3r-os is community firmware for the Polyend Tracker. It is unofficial and not affiliated with Polyend. Installing it replaces the firmware in your unit. It is licensed GPL-3.0-or-later. Third-party licences are in [THIRD_PARTY.md](../../THIRD_PARTY.md).

**Back up your SD card and keep the official firmware file before you install.**

## Read this first

- **Keys that moved.** Text entry and the Navigator now accept with `F1` and cancel with `F8`. The encoder assign is `F7` ENC in the Learn dialog (open it with `Vol`+`Fx`); the `Instr`+`Fx` chord is gone. The pad modes are in the `Shift`+`F2` PADS picker; `Shift`+`F3`..`F7` on Pattern are free. On Song, `F2` is DUP PAT. `Shift`+`Rec` toggles LIVE; `Rec` toggles Browse and Edit.
- **Saved files.** Nothing changes: v1.3.1 reads and writes the same project, instrument and settings files as v1.3.0.
- **Modules.** The module ABI is 1.13, as in v1.3.0. TB-303, Chip, FM and SID load from `modules/instruments/` on the card (from the modules zip).
- **Going back.** v1.3.1 images end below 0xFF000 and are padded to a 4 KiB sector, as v1.3.0 images are; going back works as described in the [v1.2.0 notes](v1.2.0.md).

## What is new

- **`Shift`+`File` saves** from any screen.
- **Focus Loop**: `Shift`+`Play` with a block selected loops the block.
- **One PADS picker** on `Shift`+`F2`.
- **CHOP `Shift`+`F4` TO PAT**, and CHOP AUTO / EVEN set BEAT SLICE.
- **Sample load `Shift`+`F6` MARK**, and `Shift`+`F7`/`F8` INST -/+ on every sample screen.
- **Sample edit PLUGINS `F4` NEW INS**: a generator plugin into the first free instrument.
- **MIDI lanes follow** block copy, paste, cut, row operations and pattern copy.
- **Note pads light the scale** (in scale HIGH, out of scale LOW).

## What is fixed

- **The Learn dialog** is wide enough for all eight key labels in every theme.

## Files

- `track3r-os-v1.3.1.ptf`, the update file.
- `track3r-os-v1.3.1-modules.zip`, every external module (TB-303, Chip, FM and SID among them), the factory presets and the `.ptfx` Sample Editor plugins. Unzip at the card root.
- `track3r-os-v1.3.1-examples.zip`, demo projects, a drum kit, wavetables, an OctaMED module and the licences of the files. Unzip at the card root.
- `SHA256SUMS`, the checksums of the files.

Check your download with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`. The layout of the zips is in `tools/release/README.md`.

## Install or update

- **From track3r-os v1.3.0, v1.2.0, v1.1.0, v1.0.1 or v1.0.0 (with working pads):** copy `track3r-os-v1.3.1.ptf` into `Firmware/`, unzip the two zips at the card root, put the card in the unit, open Config, INFO and press `F8` (UPDATE).
- **From the official firmware, or when the pads are dead:** copy everything on the card to your computer; copy `track3r-os-v1.3.1.ptf` into `Firmware/` and rename the copy to `PolyendTracker_0.0.1.ptf` (it must be the only `.ptf` there); use the hidden reset button, as in Polyend's [emergency flashing page](https://polyend.ladesk.com/929798-How-do-I-perform-the-emergency-flashing-procedure); after the restart, open Config, INFO. BUILD ID should show `v1.3.1`. Then unzip the two zips at the card root.

The full steps are in the [installing guide](../manual/install.md).

## Tested on the unit

On an original Tracker, over the console, USB MIDI and USB audio, with the release candidate of this code:

- `Shift`+`File` saves; `F1` and `F8` act as OK and BACK on the overlays; the Learn dialog shows `F7` ENC with all eight key labels inside it.
- CHOP TO PAT writes the slices into the pattern; INST -/+ and MARK on the sample screens; the `Shift`+`F2` PADS picker; `Shift`+`Rec` toggles LIVE; Song `F2` DUP PAT.
- Focus Loop plays the selected block in a loop (USB audio capture).
- MIDI recording into a lane with the encoder and a Learn knob as CC letters; the encoder leaves the CC letter selector alone during the count-in; a stop during the count-in records nothing and leaves no stray note.
- Chip waves 4..11: CPU max 22.9 to 28.7 % of a block, no underrun, no clipping.
- A restart with no crash record, a save, the Perform screen, and five minutes of an 8-track project with CC automation and three synth modules at CPU max 82.7 %, no underrun.
