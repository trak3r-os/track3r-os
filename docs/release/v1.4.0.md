# track3r-os v1.4.0: release notes

v1.4.0 adds a master EQ with a bass boost on the Mixer, a pitch-bend strip and a vibrato pad on the PIANO pads, and six Sample Editor plugins of our own. It also tidies the keys: `Shift`+`File` saves from any screen, `F1` accepts and `F8` goes back on every overlay, Focus Loop loops a selected block, one `Shift`+`F2` PADS picker replaces five pad chords, CHOP writes its slices into the pattern, and the encoder assign moves into the Learn dialog. The full list is in the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md).

track3r-os is community firmware for the Polyend Tracker. It is unofficial and not affiliated with Polyend. Installing it replaces the firmware in your unit. It is licensed GPL-3.0-or-later. Third-party licences are in [THIRD_PARTY.md](../../THIRD_PARTY.md).

**Back up your SD card and keep the official firmware file before you install.**

## Highlights

- **MST EQ**: a sixth Mixer page (press `Master` to turn to it) with a five-band master EQ (115 Hz, 330 Hz, 990 Hz, 3 kHz, 9.9 kHz, each -12 to +12 dB) and **BASS BOOST** (0 to 100). It runs in the codec and is saved with the project. It acts on the headphone and line outputs only, not on USB audio, renders or resampled takes. Public issue #6.
- **PIANO pads**: a pitch-bend strip (columns 10 and 11: +2, +1, -1, -2 semitones while held), bend recording into the pattern as Slide FX, MIDI pitch-wheel bend on sample voices, and a vibrato pad (column 12, rows 2 and 3).
- **Six Sample Editor plugins of our own** in the modules zip: WT Forge, WT Notes, Grain Lock, Pad Forge, WT Resynth and Pad Stretch. A WAVETABLE plugin result now sets WT SIZE.
- **`Shift`+`File` saves** from any screen; **Focus Loop** (`Shift`+`Play` with a block selected); **one PADS picker** on `Shift`+`F2`.
- **CHOP `Shift`+`F4` TO PAT**, sample load `Shift`+`F6` MARK, `Shift`+`F7`/`F8` INST -/+ on every sample screen, and PLUGINS `F4` NEW INS.
- **MIDI lanes follow** block edits, pattern copy, EXPAND, SHRINK and TRANSPOSE.
- **New projects start in C major.** Note pads light the scale.
- **Fixed:** played CC letters no longer touch the project (a save during a sweep works), and the Learn dialog fits all eight key labels.

## Read this first

- **Keys that moved.** Text entry and the Navigator now accept with `F1` and cancel with `F8`. The encoder assign is `F7` ENC in the Learn dialog (open it with `Vol`+`Fx`); the `Instr`+`Fx` chord is gone. The pad modes are in the `Shift`+`F2` PADS picker; `Shift`+`F3`..`F7` on Pattern are free. On Song, `F2` is DUP PAT. `Shift`+`Rec` toggles LIVE; `Rec` toggles Browse and Edit.
- **Saved files.** A project whose master EQ or bass boost is not flat gains an optional `MEQ ` chunk. It is written only when a value is not 0, so a project that never used MST EQ is the same file as before. Older versions keep the chunk, play with a flat output and write it back unchanged. Nothing else in the project, instrument and settings files changes from v1.3.0.
- **MST EQ does not lower the level.** Boosting a band or the bass can make a loud mix clip.
- **Modules.** The module ABI is 1.13, as in v1.3.0. TB-303, Chip, FM and SID load from `modules/instruments/` on the card (from the modules zip). Unzip the v1.4.0 modules zip to get the six new plugins in `Effects/`.
- **Going back.** v1.4.0 images end below 0xFF000 and are padded to a 4 KiB sector, as v1.3.0 images are; going back works as described in the [v1.2.0 notes](v1.2.0.md).

## Install or update

- **From track3r-os v1.3.0, v1.2.0, v1.1.0, v1.0.1 or v1.0.0 (with working pads):** copy `track3r-os-v1.4.0.ptf` into `Firmware/`, unzip the two zips at the card root, put the card in the unit, open Config, INFO and press `F8` (UPDATE).
- **From the official firmware, or when the pads are dead:** copy everything on the card to your computer; copy `track3r-os-v1.4.0.ptf` into `Firmware/` and rename the copy to `PolyendTracker_0.0.1.ptf` (it must be the only `.ptf` there); use the hidden reset button, as in Polyend's [emergency flashing page](https://polyend.ladesk.com/929798-How-do-I-perform-the-emergency-flashing-procedure); after the restart, open Config, INFO. BUILD ID should show `v1.4.0`. Then unzip the two zips at the card root.

The full steps are in the [installing guide](../manual/install.md).

## Tested on the unit

On an original Tracker, over the console, USB MIDI and USB audio, with the release candidates of this code:

- `Shift`+`File` saves; `F1` and `F8` act as OK and BACK on the overlays; the Learn dialog shows `F7` ENC with all eight key labels inside it.
- CHOP TO PAT writes the slices into the pattern; INST -/+ and MARK on the sample screens; the `Shift`+`F2` PADS picker; `Shift`+`Rec` toggles LIVE; Song `F2` DUP PAT.
- Focus Loop plays the selected block in a loop (USB audio capture).
- MIDI recording into a lane with the encoder and a Learn knob as CC letters; the encoder leaves the CC letter selector alone during the count-in; a stop during the count-in records nothing and leaves no stray note.
- MST EQ: the sixth Mixer page, flat by default, 1 dB band steps and 1-step bass steps with the limits at -12/+12 and 0/100, save and reload; the bands and the bass boost heard on the analogue output; USB audio carries no EQ (all bands at +/-12 dB and bass at 100 differ from flat by 0.14 dB or less), with live edits while playing and no underrun.
- PIANO bend strip: against a 500 Hz note, +2 gives 561.2 Hz, +1 529.7 Hz, -1 471.9 Hz and -2 445.4 Hz; the pitch returns on release; the LEDs light correctly; no change in CPU.
- Wavetable envelope shapes (now computed with a smaller maths library): no silent output, no clipping, and the same channel and master levels as the build before it, with the same CPU numbers and no underrun on the same projects.
- Chip waves 4..11: CPU max 22.9 to 28.7 % of a block, no underrun, no clipping.
- A restart with no crash record, a save, the Perform screen, and five minutes of an 8-track project with CC automation and three synth modules at CPU max 82.7 %, no underrun.
