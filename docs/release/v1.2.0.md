# track3r-os v1.2.0: release notes

v1.2.0 adds insert effects with a Mixer page, a set of effect modules, module presets, a selectable render rate for instrument modules, Sample Editor plugins (`.ptfx`), MIDI Time Code, wavetable envelopes, more Chip waves, custom play orders, a new set of Pattern tools and a reworked Song TIMELINE. Module import now needs a file from the content zip. The full list is in the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md); the earlier notes are [v1.1.0.md](v1.1.0.md), [v1.0.1.md](v1.0.1.md) and [v1.0.0.md](v1.0.0.md).

track3r-os is community firmware for the Polyend Tracker. It is unofficial and not affiliated with Polyend. Installing it replaces the firmware in your unit. It is licensed GPL-3.0-or-later. Third-party licences are in [THIRD_PARTY.md](../../THIRD_PARTY.md).

**Back up your SD card and keep the official firmware file before you install.**

## Read this first

- **Going back.** v1.2.0 images are padded to a whole 4 KiB flash sector, as v1.1.0 images are. v1.0.1 and v1.0.0 are not. **To go back to them through the Update screen** (Config, INFO, `F8`), pick the file: the screen says NOT PADDED. TRACK3R-OS WILL PAD A COPY OF IT? Press `F1` (**PAD**). The unit writes `pad-<name>.ptf` next to the file, checks it, and then offers to install it as usual. Your file is not changed. From a computer, `o3console.py --pad` does the same. The hidden reset button, with the older file alone in `Firmware/`, works as before (see the [installing guide](../manual/install.md)). Whether the Update screen takes an official Polyend file has not been checked; use the reset button for the way back to the official firmware.
- **New: an image must end at or below 0xFF000.** An image that reached the last 4 KiB flash sector (end 0x100000) hung a unit after the update: it handed over to the bootloader and did not come back. The update checker of the unit now refuses such an image, and so does the image check of the build. The v1.2.0 `.ptf` ends below the limit. If you build your own image, build it as a release build.
- **Module import needs a file.** MOD, S3M and MED import now runs from `modules/importers/tracker.tmod`, which is in the content zip. Without it IMPORT refuses and your open project is untouched. Unzip the modules zip at the card root.
- **Instrument files.** A project that uses wavetable envelopes, Play Thru or a module RATE is saved with a newer instrument version (INST 1.6, 1.7, 1.8). It opens in v1.1.0 but loses those values on its next save there. A project that uses none of them is written byte for byte as before. Projects saved by v1.1.0 open in v1.2.0.

## What is new

- **Insert effects.** The Mixer has an INSERTS page: two inserts a track and one on the master. Pick an effect, open its parameters with `F6`, bind them with MIDI Learn. Inserts are saved with the project. A CPU guard forces a heavy insert to dry instead of letting the audio underrun.
- **Effect modules on the card:** DRIVE, CRUSH, COMP (with a TRANSIENT mode and a sidechain KEY from another track), ENV FILTER, FOLD, PHASER, BIQUAD, and the stereo PING-PONG (tempo-synced), WIDE, CHORUS and AUTOPAN, with factory presets. An effect can be stereo and can read another track's level.
- **Module presets.** Shift+F5 SAVE writes a preset to the card; the F5 list has a CARD section, `F1` loads it. Factory presets for TB-303, FM, Chip, SID and the effects are in the content zip.
- **RATE for instrument modules**, FULL, 1/2 or 1/4, on the Shift+F2 MIX page: each sample is held, the sound gets crunchy and cheaper to run. It is saved with the instrument. TB-303, FM, Chip, SID and Sine offer it. MIX also has PANNING, DELAY SEND and REVERB SEND for module instruments.
- **Chip has eight more waves** and a fourth page. The old waves sound as before.
- **Sample Editor plugins.** A PLUGINS page lists `Effects/*.ptfx`, shows their parameters and applies them with undo. A plugin that faults, hangs or crashes is contained and quarantined. Thirteen come in the content zip (Add Silence, Analog Kick Gen, Chiptune Voice Gen, Multi-Mode Filter, Stereo Delay, Stereo to Mono, Overdrive, Delay, Bitcrusher, Chorus, Flanger, Limiter, three-band EQ).
- **Wavetable envelopes and LFOs.** WT ENV and WT LFO sources on the MOD page, a contour drawing, user contours from `/Envelopes/*.wav`, granular length as a destination, and step effects `w`, `y`, `z`.
- **Play Thru** for Slice and Beat Slice, and **Chance** conditions 101 to 103+ (group with last, not last, play N or skip M), as in the official file format.
- **Perform: custom play orders C1 to C20** in PLAYMODE.
- **MIDI Time Code.** MTC OUT and MTC IN on Config, so a DAW that follows MTC can follow the unit. The MIDI clock follow is tighter after tempo jumps and SPP.
- **MIDI event list** on Pattern (EVENTS), the official MIDI **CC map**, a Learn that follows the selected instrument, and the encoder as a Learn target (Instr+Fx).
- **Pattern tools.** Select, then `F7`, then a verb; one panel with the verb menu and TOOLS; TRANSPOSE and INSTRUMENT dialogs; `F6` repeats the last FILL or TOOLS apply. The selection grows in stages with Shift+Up, and Shift+Delete follows JUMP.
- **Song TIMELINE** is editable (cursor, rectangle, PREVIEW with `F1`, ADD EMPTY slot, a loop of a range); ORDER and ARRANGEMENT are folded into LIST.
- **Apps.** One Apps list on Shift+Pattern; the Navigator opens with Master+Song or Song+Pattern.
- **tracker-ce files.** A folder from Polyend's published tracker-ce loads with a strip that names what the OS lost; its 16-track and version 6 pattern files are read (read only); 39 scales on the held-Note panel with the official numbering.
- **Record:** a COUNT-IN row and status chips (MONITOR, REC, OVERDUB, LOOP). **Config:** the status LED heartbeat can be turned off.
- **Master scratch** follows the hand better (held speed, inertia, a gentler gate near standstill).

Credits: the CHIP waves, the custom play orders, the wavetable contours and the `.ptfx` plugins come from Polyend's published tracker-ce source (MIT, with the notices in [THIRD_PARTY.md](../../THIRD_PARTY.md)).

## What is fixed

- **A loaded project keeps its tempo.** A project saved with no instrument opened at 120 BPM. Fixed and covered by a test.
- **The last flash sector.** The unit's validator refuses an image that reaches it (see Read this first).
- **A `.ptfx` whose init is at payload offset 0** is a valid entry, and the console names the reason when it refuses a plugin.
- **An export carries no live effect tails**, and a project open or NEW clears the old toasts.
- **Module pages:** a module with no parameters no longer shows a useless F1 page key.

## Files

- `track3r-os-v1.2.0.ptf`, the update file. For a first install from the official firmware you rename your copy on the card (see Install).
- `track3r-os-v1.2.0-modules.zip`, every external module: the apps, the effects, the `sine` instrument, the `tracker` importer for MOD, S3M and MED import (**required for import**), the factory presets, and the `.ptfx` Sample Editor plugins (they go in `Effects/`). Unzip at the card root.
- `track3r-os-v1.2.0-examples.zip`, demo projects, a drum kit, wavetables, an OctaMED module and the licences of the files. Unzip at the card root.
- `SHA256SUMS`, the checksums of the three files.

Check your download with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`. The zips put files under `modules/apps/`, `modules/effects/`, `modules/instruments/`, `modules/importers/`, `modules/presets/<module>/`, `Effects/`, `Samples/`, `WTABLE/`, `Songs/` and `LICENSES/`; the layout is in `tools/release/README.md`.

**Module ABI.** It is now 1.13 (v1.1.0 was 1.10): 1.11 adds importers (first-party only), 1.12 stereo effects, the tempo, a sidechain KEY and presets, 1.13 the per-instrument RATE. Old `.tmod` files of ABI 1.0 to 1.10 still load. A module built for 1.13 on an older OS is refused with NEEDS NEWER OS. The SDK is in `module/sdk/`.

## Install or update

- **From track3r-os v1.1.0, v1.0.1 or v1.0.0 (with working pads):** copy `track3r-os-v1.2.0.ptf` into `Firmware/`, unzip the two zips at the card root, put the card in the unit, open Config, INFO and press `F8` (UPDATE).
- **From the official firmware, or when the pads are dead:**
  1. Copy everything on the SD card to your computer.
  2. Copy `track3r-os-v1.2.0.ptf` into `Firmware/` and rename the copy to `PolyendTracker_0.0.1.ptf`. It must be the only `.ptf` there. Do not use the Polyend Tool app.
  3. Use the hidden reset button, as in Polyend's [emergency flashing page](https://polyend.ladesk.com/929798-How-do-I-perform-the-emergency-flashing-procedure).
  4. After the restart, open Config, INFO. BUILD ID should show `v1.2.0`. Then unzip the content zips at the card root.
- **Going back:** on the Update screen with **PAD**, or by the reset button with the older file, as in Read this first.

The full steps are in the [installing guide](../manual/install.md).

## Known issues

- **Play after Stop can be lost.** On the unit, a Play tap right after a Stop tap is lost every other time. Press Play again. The cause is not found (it does not reproduce on the host).
- Swapping the SD card can crash the unit. Switch the unit off before you change the card.
- **One unexplained return to the official firmware.** During an unattended test run of a development image the unit once came back running the official firmware. It did not repeat in a later 10-minute run. The cause is open.
- Very heavy projects can exceed the CPU budget. The stereo inserts cost the most (CHORUS measured 5.4 % of a block on the unit); some Perform effects cost CPU while held. See the [CPU budget](../architecture/cpu-budget.md).
- Master scratch needs a hand on the platter to judge; it may still need tuning.
- A cancelled effect leaves the free sample memory lower until the next effect reuses the buffer.
- On macOS and Windows the USB outputs are not checked.
- `.ptfx` plugins that need the whole selection or the project tempo (Wavetable Smoother, Cut, Time-stretch of tracker-ce) are not provided.
