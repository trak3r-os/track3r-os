# OS-MIDIBEND — Pitch bend from a MIDI keyboard

Status: specified 2026-11-05, decisions taken the same day (Q1 a, Q2 a), not implemented · Level C (the voices' pitch in the audio render, the render's MIDI input path, a device setting appended to `settings.o3st`) · Backlog row OS-MIDIBEND, approved by the maintainer 2026-11-05 with BEND RANGE in, queued the same day · Facts from `dev` at c1dc3125 and `polyend/tracker-ce` at 7fd6c2c · Everything this spec proposes is **new Community OS behaviour**: the official firmware takes no pitch bend in (fact F1).

## 1. Goal

Moving the pitch wheel of a MIDI keyboard bends, live, the notes that keyboard plays on the unit, up to BEND RANGE semitones each way: the sampler voices (every Modern play mode) and the SD module instruments that export `pitch` (ABI 1.8). Notes that sound when the wheel moves follow it, and a note started while the wheel is off centre starts bent. The bend is not recorded, not saved in the project and not exported; it goes back to centre at Stop, at an all-notes-off (the panic) and at a MIDI reset. Amiga voices, modules without `pitch`, pads, the preview and app notes do not bend.

## 3. Design

### 3.1 Where the bend lives: one context, the render

The bend value never crosses a context. The interrupts keep stamping bytes into the existing rings (no ISR change). The render parses the message (`CcInput::feed`, inside `MidiLink::step`), filters it, keeps the value, and the Engine reads it in the same block through a new optional hook `EventSource::bend` (render, once a block, after `play_live`), smooths it and applies it to the voices before they render. No new ring, no new shared variable, no `volatile`: parse, keep, read and apply are one context. The only data that cross contexts are the three settings MIDI IN, IN CHANNEL and BEND RANGE, which travel main loop -> render in `MidiConfig` through its existing `SeqLockBox` (single writer, the render never waits). A bend is therefore heard in the block after its bytes are drained (1.45 ms), not after a main-loop pass as notes are (F3).

### 3.2 Parse and filter (`midi/cc_in.h`)

- `CcParser::next` learns `0xE0`: a new `ChannelMsg::Kind::Bend` (`cc` = the LSB, `value` = the MSB), with running status as for the others. Controller 121 becomes `ChannelMsg::Kind::Reset` (still never a control and never a note message: neither ring gets it). Poly pressure and the other channel mode controllers stay dropped.
- `CcInput` (render-only additions): `set_in_filter(in_ports, in_channel)` (the MIDI IN setting 0 DIN / 1 USB / 2 both, IN CHANNEL 0 All / 1..16; defaults 2 and 0, which accept everything, so a `CcInput` without a link behaves as the device defaults), `bend()` (the last accepted value, `int16` -8192..8191), `reset_bend()`. In `feed`: an accepted Bend stores `(msb << 7 | lsb) - 8192`; an accepted AllOff (120, 123; it still goes to the notes' ring as today) or Reset (121) sets it to 0. Nothing new goes into either ring (the CC ring and the notes' ring see exactly what they saw: F2's tests hold).
- The accept predicate is the notes' (F3): it moves to `midi::in_accepts` (`midi/include/o3os/midi/in_filter.h`, constexpr) and `app::midi_in_accepts` forwards to it, so the notes and the bend can never be filtered differently.
- One value for every accepted channel (Q2a).

### 3.3 The value the render works with (`seq/midi_link.cpp`)

`MidiConfig` gains `in_ports`, `in_channel`, `bend_range` (sanitized: 0..2, 0..16, 0..24), filled by `sync_config_from_settings`; the platform adds IN CHANNEL and BEND RANGE to the generations it watches. `MidiLink::apply_config` calls `cc_->set_in_filter`. The hook `bend` returns the target in 1/65536 semitone: `bend() x bend_range x 8` (exact integers; `+8191` is `range x 8191/8192`, the bend out's symmetric convention, F12; at most 1,572,864 in magnitude). `MidiLink` centres (`cc_->reset_bend()`) on: the sequencer's `Stopped` event in `step` (beside `stop_all`), a `LiveKind::AllOff` of `kLiveSourceMidi` or of every source in `live_event` (a pads' all-off does not centre), and `resume()` (after an export; main loop with the render parked, the existing protocol). BEND RANGE 0 makes the target 0.

### 3.4 Smoothing (`audio/src/engine.cpp`)

The target moves in steps (each message, at most a block apart; a 7-bit keyboard steps 1/32 semitone at range 2 and 3/8 of a semitone at range 24). The Engine keeps `bend_at_`, the value at the start of the block. Each block: `T = source_.bend(self)` (0 without the hook); `next = bend_at_ + (T - bend_at_) / 4` (towards zero, never past T), and `next = T` when |T - bend_at_| <= 64 (1/1024 semitone). The sampler voices play the block at `bend_at_` (the block's first sample, the convention of every pitch ramp here, F7); the modules get `bend_at_` and the slope that reaches `next` at the block's end. Then `bend_at_ = next`. A one-pole of a quarter a block is a time constant of about 5 ms (95 % in about 15 ms, exact landing within about 50 ms), so a 7-bit step is spread over ten blocks; the constants are tuned by ear in S5 (assumption A-4). Only the gate is hot: one indirect call and one compare in `play_block` (`T != bend_at_ || bend_moving_`); everything else is `Engine::bend_block()`, `O3OS_AUDIO_COLD`, once a block while the bend moves, plus one block after it stops (so the modules get their slope 0), named in `hot.h`'s exception list.

### 3.5 Which voices bend

Each pool keeps a bit a track, `bend_tracks_` (`uint16`): set by `Engine::play_live` right after a successful `note_on` of a `kLiveSourceMidi` On (`voices_->bend_follow(track)` or `modules_->bend_follow(track)`), cleared by EVERY note start on the track (`VoicePool` in `mods_start`, `ModuleVoices` in `start_note`), whatever its source: a sequencer note, a pad, the preview or an app note that takes the track makes it unbent; a voice stolen for another track takes that track's bit at its new note. The bit outlives the note's release, so the release tail follows the wheel as on a synth. The keyboard's notes are already filtered by MIDI IN and IN CHANNEL (F3), so "a note started from MIDI in on the IN CHANNEL" is exactly a live note of `kLiveSourceMidi`. `bend_follow` makes the note take the bend at once when it is not 0: a note started while the wheel is off centre sounds bent from its first sample. Amiga notes, module instruments without `pitch` and the tracks of pads are never marked.

### 3.6 Sampler voices (`audio/src/voices.cpp`)

`VoicePool` keeps `bend_q16_` (the value of this block) and `bend_tracks_`. The bend is added in ONE place, `repitch()`: the offset it plays is the ramp's `slide_q16` (rounded first when the glissando applies: the bend is never rounded) plus `bend_q16_` when the voice's track bit is set; every writer of `slide_q16` stays as it is, and every path that makes the pitch (`mod_block`, the Slide FX's end, a live tune edit, the finetune modulator) goes through `repitch`, so all of them include the bend. `bend_to(b)` (cold, once a block while the bend moves): when b changed, every playing voice whose track bit is set gets `kMmSlide | mneed`, so `mod_block` makes its pitch at the block's first span; `mod_block` already lets `kMmSlide` go once the ramp has landed. With no bend (0, or no bit) the offset is unchanged, so every existing sound is bit for bit the same. The Voice does not grow (F8). Bound: |Glide 127 + TUNE 48 + bend 24| semitones = 199 x 4096 < 2^20 (a `static_assert`); the pitch is held at the table's ends as today.

### 3.7 Module instruments (`audio/src/module_voices.cpp`)

The offset a bent instance plays is the ramp's (when one is walking or held) plus the bend. `bend_follow(track)`: after the note's `note_on` (which reset the module's offset), one `pitch(base + bend, slope)` call. `bend_to(from, to)` (cold, once a block while the bend moves, before the modules render): for each active instance with `pitch` on a bent track whose ramp is not walking, one `pitch(base + from, (to - from) / (64 x 65536))` call; a walking ramp's own `ramp_call` adds the bend and its slope instead (one call a block, never two); `ramp_snap` lands on the bend, not on 0. An instance that releases (active, no longer held) still follows. No ABI change: `pitch()` is the offset from the note, as ABI 1.8 says; the PITCH comment in `o3module.h` gains one sentence (the host also moves the offset for the pitch bend of a note played from MIDI in), the ABI version stays.

### 3.8 What else holds

- **Additive in semitones:** Slide FX, Glide, Perform TUNE, the tracker FX's pitch offset, fine tune and its modulator, and the bend add; nothing replaces anything.
- **Not recorded:** the bend never reaches the main loop, so a live or step recording cannot see it; nothing in the project.
- **Export:** `Engine::offline_enter` centres (`bend_at_ = 0`, both pools' bend 0 and bits clear); the export's source has no bend hook; after `offline_leave` the live link's `resume()` has centred the parser, so the wheel takes effect again at its next move.
- **Real time:** render only, bounded (one parse step a byte; at most 12 voices and 8 module instances visited a block, and only while the bend moves), no allocation, no SD, no `double`, integer smoothing.

### 3.9 Settings and UI

**BEND RANGE**, device setting, appended by the documented rule (F11): 0..24 semitones, default 2 (Q1a; `kMidiPbRangeMax`, `kMidiPbRangeDefault`), learnable like the MIDI page's other rows, not a project setting, no `.o3pj` change. Its id is the next free one when S1 merges: 26 if before OS-MTC A3 (MTC's ids then become 27..29), 29 after. The scope is full, so whichever of S1 and OS-MTC A3 merges first raises `Settings::kMaxFields` and `kValueBytes` 26 -> 32 (MTC's numbers) with `kModelArenaBytes` and its mirror; the second takes the room. Default file 246 -> 254 bytes (alone). Config MIDI page: the last row, after OUT CHANNEL (and after MTC's rows when both exist); `kConfigRows` 9 -> 10 (or MTC's 12 -> 13). Info line: "PITCH WHEEL: SEMITONES EACH WAY, 0 OFF".

## 6. Assumptions (decided here; each local, testable and reversible)

- A-1 The bend is parsed, kept, smoothed and applied in the render; only MIDI IN, IN CHANNEL and BEND RANGE cross from the main loop, in `MidiConfig`'s `SeqLockBox`. No ISR change, no new ring, no `volatile`.
- A-2 The bend's filter is the notes' filter (one predicate, `midi::in_accepts`).
- A-3 Mapping: `(value - 8192) x range / 8192` semitones, symmetric (the bend out's convention); full up is range x 8191/8192.
- A-4 Smoothing: a quarter of the remaining distance a block, landing within 1/1024 semitone; the sampler holds the value of the block's start, a module gets the slope across the block. Tuned by ear in S5.
- A-5 Which notes: live notes of `kLiveSourceMidi` on the sampler or a module with `pitch`, by a bit a track per pool, cleared by any later note start on the track, kept through the release.
- A-6 A note started while the wheel is off centre starts bent.
- A-7 Centre at: `Stopped`, an all-notes-off of the MIDI source or of every source, an accepted CC 120, 121 or 123, `MidiLink::resume()`, BEND RANGE 0. Centring is smoothed like any change. A pads' all-off does not centre.
- A-8 Not recorded, not in the project, not in an export (`offline_enter` centres).
- A-9 The bend adds to every other pitch source; the glissando rounds the ramp only; held at the pitch table's ends (sampler) and ±128 semitones (module ABI).
- A-10 No bend for Amiga voices, modules without `pitch`, MIDI instruments, pads, the preview and app notes.
- A-11 BEND RANGE: a device setting, learnable, the next free id at merge (26 before OS-MTC A3, else 29); the raise 26 -> 32 by whichever merges first.
- A-12 Config MIDI page, the last row; no on-screen bend indicator.
- A-13 One bend value for all accepted channels (Q2a).

## 7. Risks

- R1 **SRAM_L** for the hot gate in `play_block` (SRAM_L has been within tens of bytes of full). Mitigation: the gate is one indirect call and one compare; if the map shows no room, read the hook inside an existing cold call (S3 reports the SRAM_L delta from the mk66 map and `check_ramfunc`).
- R2 **CPU** while a wheel moves under a 12-note chord: `repitch` per bent voice per block (estimate 0.9 % of the block). Measured by S5 (7); if over 1.5 %, repitch every second block while the bend moves (a constant).
- R3 **Zipper** at range 24 with a 7-bit keyboard: the smoothing constants (A-4); S5 (8) is listened to.
- R4 **Merge clash with OS-MTC** (ids, the raise, `kConfigRows`, the settings test literals, `mem_map.h`): the rule of 3.9; `tools/ci.sh qemu` before merge.
- R5 **Third-party modules** that ignore `pitch()` outside a Slide or Glide do not bend: allowed by the ABI; the SDK comment says the host may call it for a bend.
- R6 **A lost bend message** leaves a note slightly bent until the wheel moves again, Stop or an all-notes-off.
