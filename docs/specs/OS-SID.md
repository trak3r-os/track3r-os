# OS-SID: a SID-style instrument module (`sid.tmod`)

Level B (a new instrument module through the existing ABI 1.8; no core change, no change to Chip or any existing format). Queued by the maintainer on 2026-10-30 ("añade el modulo sid, ponlo en cola"), after the radar card `docs/vision/radar/sid-instrument.md` (option A: a new module, Chip unchanged). New Community OS behaviour: it imitates the MOS 6581 / 8580 voice. It is not an emulation and makes no parity claim. Skills: `sd-module`, `tmod-perf`; `dsp-port` for the reference rules only, since nothing is ported.

## Facts (dev 8e8d7c18)

- Chip is the structure to copy and then diverge from: `modules/chip/` (`chip.cpp`, `chip_dsp.h`, `module.json`, `tests/chip_test.cpp`, README). Its pitch runs at control rate every 32 samples: 3-step arpeggio, sweep, vibrato, and the host's slide / glide through `O3M_NOTE_RAMP`. The pulse uses PolyBLEP, the noise is a sample-and-hold of an LFSR at 16 × the note frequency, and there are two views. Measured cost: 2.3 to 2.8 % of a block per sounding voice (`docs/architecture/cpu-budget.md`).
- ABI limits (`module/sdk/o3module.h`): 32 parameters, 4 pages of 8, 16 labels per enum, 8 views. The host gives a module `exp2_f`, `log2_f`, `sin_f`, `cos_f` and the draw table, but no filter. The module therefore carries its own filter.
- The built-in modules TB-303, Chip and FM are embedded in flash through `O3M_BUILTIN_STEMS` (modules/CMakeLists.txt:184, `tools/o3m_embed.py`), and `modules/builtin/NOTICES.txt` lists them.
- FLASH is 939,548 B after OS-IMPORT-ANY (mk66 report). The `.ramfunc_l` module slab is 14 KiB and shared by all modules; Chip's hot part uses 2,096 B of it.

## Decisions (orchestrator, 2026-10-30; defaults, to confirm by the maintainer)

| # | Question | Default |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Where it lives | Built in, like Chip, TB-303 and FM: add it to `O3M_BUILTIN_STEMS` so it shows in ENGINE without copying files, and also pack it as `/modules/instruments/sid.tmod`. Flash cap 18 KB. Above that, stop for a decision (SD only). |
| S2 | Parameters | The card's 31 on four pages: OSC, ENV, FILTER, PITCH / ARP. The engineer may rename or trim parameters, but there are no new pages. The 32nd slot stays free. |
| S3 | Combined waveforms | The module's own approximate tables: 256 entries × 8 bit, for 4 combinations × 2 models (2 KiB), generated by a documented formula (the AND of the waveform bits, then smoothing) and tuned by ear. No table data, fitted parameters or constants from reSID, reSIDfp, Furnace or any emulator (GPL). It will not sound identical to a real chip. |
| S4 | Numbers | The ADSR times (16 attack times and 16 decay / release times), the register widths, the noise LFSR length and taps, and the clock (PAL 985,248 Hz) come from public primary documents: the Commodore 64 Programmer's Reference Guide and the MOS 6581 datasheet. Cite them in the README. Where a document does not settle a number, mark it as an open research question and choose by ear. GPL source is never read for numbers. |
| S5 | Filter | A zero-delay-feedback state-variable filter in the module, one pass per sample: LP, BP and HP at once, mixed by Mode (OFF, LP, BP, HP, NOTCH = LP+HP, LP+BP, BP+HP, ALL). Resonance 0..15. Model 6581 / 8580 selects the cutoff curve, the combined tables and a soft clip for the 6581. One filter per instance (per track), where the real chip shares one filter between its three voices. Document this as new Community OS behaviour. |
| S6 | Sync and ring | From an internal modulator oscillator (note plus Mod Pitch / Mod Fine). Sync is sub-sample with a BLEP on the reset. Ring multiplies the triangle by the modulator's sign. |
| S7 | Left out of v1 | The ADSR delay bug, the analogue 6581 modelling (DAC non-linearity, drift, oscillator leak), `.sid` playback, GoatTracker import (IDEA OS-GTIMPORT), and preset packs. |
| S8 | CPU | Target at most 5 % of a block per sounding voice with the filter on. Above 6 % on the unit, stop for a decision; levers are listed in the card. Measure on the unit before merge (`tmod-perf`). |

## Plan (one branch `feat/OS-SID`, two commits, merged after the unit measurement)

1. **The voice without the filter:**
   - `modules/sid/`: manifest, DSP header, `sid.cpp`, generated descriptor, tests;
   - saw, triangle, pulse with PW and PWM, 23-bit noise, combined waves, sync and ring, the stepped ADSR, the pitch block from Chip;
   - pages OSC, ENV, PITCH / ARP, and the views Wave and Envelope;
   - host tests through the ABI, as `chip_test.cpp` does: the pitch of every wave; a pulse width sweep with no clicks; noise period and level; ADSR times against the S4 table within one control step; a sync reset with no alias spike (FFT against a naive reset); ring sign; release to idle; the parameter blob round trip; `O3M_NOTE_RAMP` keeps the envelope.
2. **The filter and its page:**
   - the S5 filter, the FILTER page, the Filter view (the response curve, at most 256 points);
   - tests: stability at the highest resonance and cutoff, the −3 dB point within a tolerance of the curve, notch depth, the 6581 soft clip bounded.
- Both commits:
  - `tmod-perf`'s static disassembly check, with the hot part and its slab share recorded (it must fit next to TB-303, Chip and FM);
  - `ci.sh qemu`, because target goldens run only there;
  - the README, `modules/builtin/NOTICES.txt`, the QA rows in `docs/qa/catalogue/modules.md` (block QA-MOD-210..235), and the manual section in `docs/manual/module-sounds.md`.

## Unit (bench image from the branch, before merge)

Per `tmod-perf`, with `diag mod`:
- B1: each wave alone;
- B2: filter on and off;
- B3: sync and ring with a swept modulator;
- B4: three SID tracks held, and eight;
- by ear: the waves, the sync sweep, the filter sweep at resonance 15 on both models, the release tails.
Record the figures in `docs/architecture/cpu-budget.md`.
