## 1. Screens

Pages = what the screen's own button cycles (`screens/screen.h:30`). Shift+F belongs to the screen (OS-SHIFTROW, rule 1 below): Global binds Shift+F1..F8 to `ui.none` (`:286-294`); Song, File and Sample load bind the quarter jumps (0/4..3/4 and the end) in their own contexts. F8 UNDO / Shift+F8 REDO where listed. The Shift column is the row each screen draws while Shift is held (`Screen::soft_keys_shifted`, OS-UIFIX): what Shift+F1..F8 really do there. A key with a Shift binding shows its label; every other key is `-` and does nothing: the existing resolver order (innermost context first, then the no-modifier retry) plus Global's `ui.none` bindings end the walk, so a Shift+F press never falls through to the plain key (the retry still serves Shift+Play, `core/binding.cpp:60-70`). The rule holds for taps; a Hold with Shift may still reach a plain Hold binding (Pattern's chooser hold on F1..F3). `tests/shift_labels_test.cpp` resolves every key in every chain of every screen and fails a `-` over a bound key, a label that is not what runs and any fall-through.

Hold it: a line across the top edge fills in 2 s (from 150 ms, `Sel` on the strip, drawn after the overlays) and the strip text reads `HOLD TO SWITCH OFF`, or `UNSAVED WORK - LET GO TO KEEP IT` when the project has changes; let go and it empties (a tap of 0.15 s or more shows `HOLD POWER 2 S TO SWITCH OFF` for 1.5 s). At 2 s the unit is committed (`POWERING OFF`: the keys, pads, MIDI and the console are locked) and, in slice 1, restarts after Power is released. Shift down when Power goes down is the screenshot (OS-SCREENSHOT, `docs/specs/OS-SCREENSHOT.md`): no line, no hint; when both keys are up the first frame is saved as `Screenshots/SHOT_nnnn.bmp` (the strip says `SCREENSHOT SAVED: <name>` or `NO SCREENSHOT: <why>`, the screen freezes for 1-2 s, a key press does not stop it); Shift pressed during the hold cancels it until Power is let go. A firmware update, a running export and (when the disk is writable) a computer that may be writing refuse the first hold with a strip text (`FIRMWARE UPDATE RUNNING`, `EXPORT RUNNING - HOLD POWER AGAIN TO STOP IT`, `COMPUTER MAY BE WRITING - HOLD AGAIN`); a second hold within 10 s stops an export and powers off.

## 5. UI budget rules

1. **Invisible home first, in cost order**: a value in an existing cell (FX column, descriptor row) → a free key on the screen → a page → an overlay → a screen. A spec that skips a step says why.
2. **Modules bring generated pages**: a module's UI is its descriptor table with named pages (the ABI minor OS-INSTUI S10 claims at merge; 1.3 is ADR 0014 H1's); ≤ 5 pages × 8 rows per instance; no hand-written module page in the OS; kits sub-select with Left/Right.
3. **Counts**: ≤ 5 F pages (F1–F5), F6/F7 actions or navigation, F8 UNDO on a document screen / BACK on an overlay; ≤ 8 rows a page; press-again cycles ≤ 3, forward only, label carries `n/3`; **no cycle inside a cycle** (when an F-key cycles, the screen button does not); every screen with a Shift+F binding ships `soft_keys_shifted` (test: a Shift binding without a label fails).
4. **Every spec names its UI home** — screen, page, key, overlay, rows added — and the counts after the change against this map.
5. **This map is reviewed when a big item lands** (a new screen, overlay, page or Shift row), and its tables are regenerated from `bindings.cpp` (the OS-SYS key reference), never hand-maintained.
6. **One meaning per key class**: Copy / Shift+Copy = copy / paste; Delete = reset or clear the thing under the cursor, Shift+Delete = the larger reset; Insert = add here; pad = sound, Shift+pad = select; F1 = accept on an overlay. A deviation is labelled on the key row.
7. **Visible state**: any mode that changes what keys do is in the strip or info line (WATCH / STEP / LIVE, pads mode, LOOP, MOD n/3), and a refused key says why (maintainer override, 2026-10-14, OS-RECSWAP: Shift+arrows while the cursor follows the playhead were refused silently; OS-BUG-WATCHSEL, 2026-10-23: they now make a block there too, its end set by the arrows). A key whose meaning follows a state (REC/STOP, MOD 2/3, OVERDUB) swaps with the whole key row, and the header names the state. A dialog owns the key row (OS-KEYROWDLG): every modal overlay shows its own keys on it, every other key blank, and a question on the strip marks its answer keys, inverted, while the other keys keep their labels (`docs/specs/OS-KEYROWDLG.md`, `app/key_prompt.cpp`).
8. **One modal at a time, one level of return**: no overlay opens another; a "back to the caller" jump (S5) is one level.
9. **Shift+F belongs to the screen** (OS-SHIFTROW): Global binds Shift+F1..F8 to `ui.none`, a screen binds the Shift+F keys that mean something and the rest are blank and do nothing; Shift+F8 is REDO only where F8 is UNDO; where an object has a name, its Shift key is NAME: Shift+F1..F5 = NAME COPY PASTE DELETE SAVE (Instrument, OS-INSTUI S1), Shift+F1 on the Mixer (the track), Shift+F6 on Song (the pattern) and on File (the project's title), OS-NAMES. (Rule 5, Left/Right = previous/next page, is withdrawn: on a paged screen with a single column Left/Right edit the row, Shift + Left/Right in the coarse step, and the pages are the F-keys or the screen button again: Instrument OS-INSTUI-ARROWS, Config and Record OS-CFG-ARROWS, 2026-10-29; a module with kits keeps Left/Right for the kit.)
10. **Shift+Up/Down = one page on a list** (OS-NAV3): on a list the arrows move one row, Shift+Up / Shift+Down move one page, a page being the rows the list shows (File PROJECTS and IMPORT 16, Sample load 16, Modules 16, the engine picker 10, Learn 5, the step popup's instrument and FX lists). It clamps at the first and last entry and never wraps; a list shorter than a page goes to its ends. The rule applies only where Shift+Up/Down have no meaning of their own: Pattern and Song select a block or a range, Perform swaps (Left/Right), Chop sets the slices, Sample edit sets the amplitude and an app's page chooses a parameter row, and those keep it (pinned in `builtin_bindings_test.cpp`). The Shift row of the key strip is unchanged (it lists F keys); the gesture is listed with the Shift combinations at the head of `bindings.cpp` (and goes into the key reference when the OS-SYS generator exists).
