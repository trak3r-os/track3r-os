# OS-QANET findings, first pass (2026-10-21)

The findings of the OS-QANET test net (`docs/specs/OS-QANET.md`) that are not one of the seven known bugs, for the maintainer's triage. Per spec section 12, Q2, a finding blocks `rc/1.0.0` unless the maintainer marks it "after 1.0". Nothing here is fixed; each accepted line becomes an `OS-BUG-*` row in `docs/roadmap/BACKLOG.md`. Evidence label: **new Community OS behaviour** (test findings on the simulator); none is verified on the unit yet.

State of the net when this was written: dev 3cd779a. Merged parts: E1, E2, A1 to A4, B1, B2, C1 to C3, D1 to D4. Running: B4 (rules at every random step), C4 (the audio matrix inside the walk), F1 (planted mutants). Not started: B3, B6, B7, B8b (the voice pool, a folder of 2000, a full card). B8a (`--exhaust`: the undo cap and the sample-edit history cap) is on its branch with B5. B5 (metamorphic relations, save+OPEN among them) is on its branch. After 1.0: E3, D5.

## Limits of the net, recorded

- Rule 1 had a blind spot, narrowed by B8a (2026-10-24). With a full sample-edit history (`SampleBank::kHistory`), any silent change was credited as a unit (it hid 3 of 50 loads over a used slot in 200 seeds). Now a history that did not grow credits only a change confined to the history's own slot (its audio, its instrument); a silent change of the pattern, the song, another instrument or another slot is judged. The `--exhaust` fixture of the sample-edit cap plants such a change with the history full: the old rule missed it (`qanet_random_exhaust_selftest` fails with the old condition put back), the new one finds it. Left: a WAV load over the history slot's own audio by another path still looks like an edit at any depth 1..3, and so does an eviction for room at a partial depth (lane A owns that rule); the credit needs the history slot's audio to have changed, so an instrument-only change is judged. The first edit after a slot switch (the bank drops the other slot's history) is credited as the unit and is the third fixture (`sedit_switch`, counted in `--min-exhaust 6`; with the old before-slot rule `qanet_random_exhaust_selftest` fails it). `--exhaust` runs on seeds 1 and 2 of `qanet_random_exhaust` and in its self-test, not in the shards. The B8a fixtures also found, in the harness and in the rule, that a large sample frees the oldest history step when the arena has no copy to spare (allowed as an eviction).
- 69 actions never changed the document in any run of the A1 walk (`tests/qanet/never_changed.txt`). Rule 1 cannot fire for them until a state reaches them. The list is a tracked gap: the test fails when it grows.
- The D2 card model and the D3 limits are PROVISIONAL until the tester runs `platform/mk66/tools/hil/qanet_latency.json` on the unit (D4).
- Every finding here is on the simulator. The unit check is the maintainer's hands session.

## Residuals of the open's verify (OS-QAFIX-4, finding 4)

- The verify checks framing, chunk versions, the PHDR counts against the PATT and INST chunks, and the CRC; the content of the chunks is the Reader's. A native file with a good CRC and a field the model rejects (a hand-made file, a writer bug) still empties the document when the Reader fails after its reset. A second project does not fit the SDRAM (the arena is 1.9 MB of the 192 KB slack), so a full stage-and-swap is not possible on the unit.
- OG folders have no checksum: the verify checks `project.mt`'s header and its length against its version's layout (the Reader's own rule); the pattern and instrument files are read after the document was reset, as before. A bad OG pattern or instrument file counts as a failed file in the report, never as a failed open.
- A module import (File > IMPORT) is not verified: its importer resets the document at its first step. A file that is not a module is refused before that; a module that fails later empties the document.
- A file changed between the verify and the load is not covered; saves and the USB hand-over are held out while a load phase runs.
- Hardware check left: the render-block maximum on the unit while a verify runs (the verify reads the file on the main loop in 16 KB slices, like the load).
