# Perform: feature catalogue

One row per user-visible function of the Perform screen (`docs/ui/ui-map.md` section 1; fixed table of `docs/specs/OS-FINALQA.md` section 1). IDs are never reused. The simulator scenario is `sim/scenarios/qa/qa_perform.txt`, its goldens are the `qa_perform/qa_prf_NNN` keys of `sim/goldens.txt`. These rows come from OS-UIFIX (UI only: no unit step); the screen has no cursor, so Global's Shift + F1..F5 jumps move nothing (and shadow the scene, HALF and DOUBLE keys); Shift + F6..F8 have no Shift binding and fall through to LATCH, NEXT PAT and RESET. The rest of the screen's rows are added by the final batch.
