## The metronome chip and the count-in (OS-METRO)

COUNT-IN (Config > PATTERN: Off, 1 bar, 2 bars) applies when the transport is **stopped** and a recording is armed: Shift + Rec here (OS-RECMODE2), F1 on the Record screen with ALIGN not FREE (QA-PAT-058). The transport counts N bars on row 1 (OS-COUNTIN): the play line waits on row 1 and nothing of the pattern plays; row 1 is the first step played when the count ends. The click sounds if METRO is not Off, the strip counts the beats left, the pad LEDs flash each beat (all pads, the first beat of a bar brighter) and nothing is recorded until the start point. Focus Loop never counts in. `playhead <step> countin <steps>` in a sim scenario is what the sequencer publishes while it counts in. Host tests: `seq_metro_test.cpp` (the start step, the flags, the position, the accents), `metro_app_test.cpp` (Play, the pads, the strip, the LEDs), `record_screen_test.cpp` (the Record screen).

## The ALIGN wait of a take on a running transport (OS-METROALIGN)

With the transport **running**, a take armed on the Record screen with START ON STEP, BAR or PATTERN waits for that boundary, and there is no extra count-in: the wait is the count-in, shown the same way (the strip's `RECORDING IN 4.. 3.. 2.. 1..`, the pads' flash, the click as METRO says) when at least one beat is left at the moment the take is armed; a countdown that has begun goes on to the take's start. The steps left come from the published step and the pattern's length (Bar: the next multiple of 16 or the pattern's end, Pattern: step 0); the take itself is untouched (it starts at the same Step event, to the sample). Focus Loop (`Shift + Play`) is not a take and never counts. `playhead <step> wait <bar|pattern>` in a sim scenario says such a take is armed. Host tests: `recorder_test.cpp` (`wait_steps_left`, `wait_beat`, the start step), `record_screen_test.cpp` (the strip, the stop, the end of a count-in).
